What to look at

  1. As staff, open the switcher — prompt copy, never a blank list.
  2. Type c — nothing fires under 2 characters.
  3. Type co — sections appear. Discovered rows carry no role label: staff hold no grant of their own on them.
  4. Every row carries a Member or Non-member chip. Non-members are returned and chipped, not hidden — see Contoso Ltd and Concord Systems. Hover a Non-member chip for the finer Salesforce status.
  5. Scroll to the bottom of the results — the next page loads itself, no button, no total.
  6. Switch to administrator, reopen without typing — unsectioned, exactly as today except the chips.
  7. As administrator, type co — only your own orgs match. FGA filters every hit, so a non-staff caller discovers nothing and "All organizations" is omitted.
  8. As administrator, type initech — your lapsed org shows both signals at once: a role label and a Non-member chip.
  9. Switch to staff + org admin and open without typing — you see The Linux Foundation alone, and no hint that search reaches further. See the note below.
  10. Now type co as that persona — both sections, The Linux Foundation labelled, the rest bare.
  11. Pick any row — it pins to the trigger and the panel closes.

Open question — the mixed case

The "search for any organization" prompt only renders when the assigned list is empty. A staff member who administers even one org therefore opens the switcher, sees that one org, and gets no signal that search reaches the whole catalogue. They have to guess. Options: always show a discovery hint under the list, change the placeholder to "Search all organizations" when the caller has broader read access, or accept it.
