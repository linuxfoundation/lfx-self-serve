## How do I join a mailing list?

Open **Mailing Lists** for the project or foundation and select **Join** on the list. You are subscribed with the email address on your account. **Join** only appears on lists whose **Audience & Access** is **Public**. See [Manage Mailing Lists](../manage-mailing-lists/).

## Why is there no Join button for a list?

- The list is **Approval Required** or **Invite Only**. Someone who manages the list has to add you.
- You are already subscribed. Look for the **Member** badge next to the list's name.
- Your subscriptions didn't finish loading. Refresh the page and try again.
- You are signed in with the board member persona, which doesn't show **Join** buttons.

## How do I leave a mailing list?

Switch to your own view (**Me**), select **Mailing Lists**, and select the leave icon at the end of the row. Confirm in the **Leave Mailing List** dialog. See [Manage Mailing Lists](../manage-mailing-lists/).

## I left a list but I'm still getting email

If the list is kept in sync with a group, leaving may not persist if your role in that group changes. The leave dialog and the information icon beside the list in your **My Subscription** column warn about this. Contact the people who manage the list if it keeps coming back.

## Why don't I see any mailing lists?

- On a project or foundation, make sure the right one is selected in the project picker. Mailing lists belong to one project or foundation.
- In your own view (**Me**), you only see the lists you are subscribed to.
- The project may not have any lists yet. If it also has no mailing list service set up, you see a message to contact support.

## Who can create a mailing list?

People who can manage mailing lists for the project or foundation. The project also needs a mailing list service. If you don't see **Add Mailing List**, contact your project administrator. See [Create a Mailing List](../create-mailing-list/).

## What do the settings mean?

**Audience & Access** controls who can join (**Public**, **Approval Required**, **Invite Only**). **Posting Permission** controls how conversations work (**Open Discussion**, **Moderated Discussion**, **Announcement**). **Visibility** controls who can read past discussions (**Public** or **Subscribers only**). See [Mailing List Settings and Visibility](../mailing-list-settings/).

## Can I change a list from Subscribers only back to Public?

No. **Subscribers only** is a permanent privacy setting from Groups.io. Once a list has it, the **Public** option is turned off when you edit the list. Choose carefully when you create it.

## Why can't I make an Announcement list private?

Announcement lists must have **Public** visibility. If you choose **Announcement** with **Subscribers only**, you see "Announcement mailing lists must have public visibility." and can't continue until you change one of them.

## How do I change the mailing list name or description?

Open the list's page and select **Edit Mailing List**. You can change the description, the settings, and the linked group. The name can't be changed after the list is created. If you need a different name, create a new list. You need permission to manage the list. See [Manage Mailing Lists](../manage-mailing-lists/).

## How do I add someone to a mailing list?

Open the list's page, select **Add Subscriber**, and fill in their name, email address, delivery mode, and role. Members of any linked group are subscribed automatically. See [Manage Subscribers](../manage-subscribers/).

## Can I change a subscriber's name or email address?

No. You can change a subscriber's title and organization, their delivery mode, and their role. To fix a wrong name or email address, remove the subscriber and add them again. See [Manage Subscribers](../manage-subscribers/).

## What are Individual, Digest, and None?

They are the delivery modes. **Individual** sends messages as they are posted, **Digest** groups them together, and **None** sends the person no list email. You can change a subscriber's delivery mode in the subscriber table.

## How do I delete a mailing list?

LFX Self Serve doesn't have a delete control for mailing lists. Contact LFX support and include the list name and the project it belongs to.

## What happens to a mailing list when the project is archived?

If a project is archived, contact LFX support for information about accessing its mailing list history.

## Who do I contact if a mailing list is set up incorrectly?

Contact LFX support. Include the list name and the project it belongs to.
