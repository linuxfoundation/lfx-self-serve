Every project meeting has a join page. Opening its link takes you straight to that meeting, with no list to pick from first. Meetings run on Zoom, and the join page is where you get into the Zoom meeting, respond to your invitation, and find materials and recordings.

## Open the join page

Open the link your meeting organizer shared. It looks like `https://app.lfx.dev/meetings/{meeting-id}`. Use **Copy meeting link** if you want to save or forward it. A link to a private meeting carries the meeting's password, so share it only with people who should have it.

The page shows the meeting's title and the join button. If you have access to the meeting, it also shows the date and time, the agenda, and the materials. If you're signed in, it also shows who is organizing the meeting.

## Join the meeting

You can join from **10 minutes before the start time**, or earlier if the organizer set a longer early join time, until **40 minutes after the scheduled end**. Before then, the page says "You may only join the meeting up to N minutes before the start time," where N is the meeting's early join time. Once the meeting is open, it says "The meeting is in progress."

### If you're signed in

Select **Join Meeting**. When the meeting opens, you're taken to Zoom automatically, using the details from your LFX account.

If you see an error about your email address, such as that you aren't on the guest list for a restricted meeting, select **Click here** to join using a different email address, and use the email the organizer invited.

### If you're not signed in

You don't need an LFX account to join a Private meeting. Open the link the organizer shared, because it includes the meeting's password. Fill in the **Enter your information** form with your **Full Name**, **Email**, and **Organization**, then select **Join Meeting**.

A **Restricted** meeting is different. There's no form to get past it: you need to **sign in**, and your account has to match someone on the meeting's guest list. If it doesn't, ask the organizer to add you, then sign in again.

To join quickly with your saved information, use **Sign In** and **Sign in with your LFX account**.

## What you can see on the page

What the page shows depends on the meeting's access settings and on who you are:

- **A public meeting with no join restriction**: everyone sees the full details, the agenda, and the date and time.
- **A Private or Restricted meeting**: people who are invited, and organizers, see the full details after signing in. Anyone else who opens a link that includes the meeting's password sees "This is a private meeting," with the title and what they need to join. They don't see the agenda, materials, or the date and time, even if they're signed in. Someone who isn't invited and opens a link without the password sees **Meeting Not Found**.

The organizer's name and contact link are only shown to signed-in visitors, even on a public meeting.

If the organizer turned on **Show attendees to guests**, invited people can open **Show Members** to see who else is invited. Without it, they see how many people are invited but not who.

## Register for a meeting

If a meeting is public and not restricted, and you haven't already been invited, a signed-in visitor sees a **Register for Meeting** button. Selecting it opens a short form: **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** are required, and **Job Title** and **Organization** are optional. Submitting it adds you as a registrant.

The button doesn't appear if you aren't signed in, if you're already invited, or if the meeting is Restricted. For a Restricted meeting, ask the organizer to add you.

## Respond to an invitation (RSVP)

If you've been invited to a meeting that collects RSVPs, the join page and your meeting card show **Yes**, **Maybe**, and **No** buttons. You need to be signed in. Your current response shows as **Going**, **Maybe**, or **Not going**. Select a different answer at any time to change it. **Change response** moves keyboard focus to your selected answer without saving anything.

For a recurring meeting, **This date or all dates?** asks which dates your response applies to:

- **This date only**: only the date shown. Other dates keep their responses.
- **All dates**: the entire series.
- **This date and all following dates**: this date and future dates. Earlier dates keep their responses.

Your response and the dates it covers are shown after you save. Choosing **No** doesn't remove the meeting from your Meetings list. You can find it under **All meetings** or **Not going**. See [Meetings Dashboard and Filters](../dashboard/).

## Resend your invitation

If you can't find your invitation email, select **Resend Invite** on the join page or on your meeting card. It sends the invitation to your own email address.

## Remove yourself from a meeting

If you're registered for an upcoming meeting and can't attend, select **Remove Myself** on the meeting's card or join page. You need to be signed in.

- Your registration ends, and your saved response is discarded. If you register again, you start with no response.
- If you were added through a group or a mailing list, you stay a member of it. Its syncs won't add you back to this meeting unless you leave and rejoin the group or list. An organizer, or you registering yourself, can still add you.
- Organizers who are also registered can remove their registration and stay organizers.
- If only some of your registrations can be removed, you see **Removal incomplete**. You stay invited, and you can try again.

## Meeting materials

The join page shows a **Meeting Materials** panel with files (**Primary Materials**) and links (**Supporting Materials**). You need to be signed in and have access to the meeting to see them, and a private meeting's materials aren't shown to someone who joined with only its password link. On a recurring meeting, each item is labelled **This occurrence** or **All occurrences**. See [Meeting Materials](../meeting-materials/).

## After the meeting

Once the meeting has ended, the page says "This meeting has ended" and can show a **Meeting Tools** panel, "provided for accessibility and reference":

- **See Recording** opens the recording.
- **AI Summary** opens the summary, with an **Approved** or **Pending Review** badge. If there's no summary, it shows "AI Summary Not Available."
- **View Transcript** opens the transcript. If there's none, it shows "Transcript Unavailable."

You need to be signed in and have access to the meeting. The organizer's **Who can access recordings & summaries** setting also decides who can use them: **Meeting Hosts Only**, **Meeting Guests**, or **Public**. See [Past Meetings](../past-meetings/) for who can see them and how recordings, summaries, and attendance work.

## Meeting not found

If you see the **Meeting Not Found** page, it's usually one of these: the link has expired or is no longer valid, the meeting was deleted or cancelled, you need access to a restricted meeting, or there's a typo in the URL. Contact the meeting organizer for a correct link, or use the **Contact Support** button on that page.
