## Who can create meetings?

Users with **maintainer**, **board-member**, or **executive-director** personas can create and manage meetings for their projects. A project can also grant an individual **Meeting Coordinator** access specifically for meetings, which lets them create and manage meetings for that project even if they don't otherwise hold one of those personas. Contributors without that grant can view meeting details but cannot create or edit meetings. See [Schedule a Meeting](../schedule-meeting/).

## Which video platform do meetings use?

Zoom. Meetings run on Zoom, and the join page takes you into the Zoom meeting.

## What's the difference between an organizer and a meeting host?

An **organizer** manages the meeting: they can edit, cancel, or delete it, manage its guests and materials, and reconcile attendance. An organizer is always a host too. A **Meeting Host** is a guest the organizer marked as a host. They can see the Zoom host key and run the meeting, but they can't edit it. See [Host Controls and the Host Key](../host-controls/).

## Does an attendee need an LFX account to join a meeting?

It depends on the meeting's access settings.

- **Public meetings** have a join page anyone can open, and you can join without an account. You need to sign in to see materials and recordings.
- **Private meetings** can be joined without an account too. Open the link the organizer shared, because it includes the meeting's password.
- **Restricted meetings** need you to sign in, and your account has to match someone on the guest list.

The organizer's name and contact link on the join page are only shown to signed-in visitors. See [Join a Meeting](../join-meeting/).

## Can I protect a meeting with a passcode?

There's no passcode field you type in. **Private** meetings have a password, but it's built into the join link when the organizer copies it, so you don't enter it separately. To control exactly who gets in, choose **Invited guests only** when scheduling. That makes the meeting **Restricted**, so only signed-in guests on the list, organizers, and linked group members can join. If you need access to a restricted meeting, ask the organizer to add you, then sign in.

## What is the AI agenda generation feature?

When creating or editing a meeting, you can fill in a title, meeting type, and a short prompt, then use AI assistance to generate a draft agenda. Review and edit the suggestion before saving, because it's a starting point, not a final agenda. The **Agenda Templates** panel also offers pre-built templates for your meeting type. See [Schedule a Meeting](../schedule-meeting/).

## Can I schedule recurring meetings?

Yes. Turn on the repeat option when scheduling, then choose a cadence: daily, weekly, every weekday, or monthly on a day like the second Tuesday. **Custom** lets you repeat every N days, weeks, or months, pick specific weekdays, and end the series on a date or after a number of occurrences. You can later edit, cancel, or add materials to a single occurrence. See [Schedule a Meeting](../schedule-meeting/) and [Manage Meetings](../manage-meetings/).

## How do I add meetings to my calendar?

There's no button to download a single meeting as its own ICS file. Instead, from the **Meetings** dashboard in your Foundation or Project lens, use **Subscribe** to get a live calendar feed for that project's or foundation's meetings, then add it to Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple Calendar. The feed excludes **Private** meetings. See [Meetings Dashboard and Filters](../dashboard/).

## What happens when I open a meeting link and see "Meeting Not Found"?

This can happen if the link has expired or is no longer valid, the meeting was deleted or cancelled, the meeting is restricted and you don't have access, or there's a typo in the URL. Contact your meeting organizer for a correct link, or use the **Contact Support** option on that page.

## Why can't I see a meeting I was invited to?

Make sure you're in the right lens. Your personal **Meetings** view (Me lens) shows meetings across all your projects, while a project's **Meetings** page (Project lens) only shows that project's meetings. Use the lens switcher to move between them. Also check your filters, and select **Reset filters** to clear them. If you only have the join link, you can open it directly in your browser. See [Meetings Dashboard and Filters](../dashboard/).

## Can I register myself for a meeting I wasn't invited to?

Yes, if the meeting is **public and not restricted**, and you're **signed in**. Its join page shows a **Register for Meeting** button with a short form for your name, email, and optionally your job title and organization. This button isn't available for **Restricted** meetings. For those, ask the organizer to add you as a guest. See [Join a Meeting](../join-meeting/).

## Can I RSVP to a meeting I'm invited to?

Yes, if you're **signed in** and the meeting collects RSVPs. Choose **Yes**, **Maybe**, or **No**. For a recurring meeting, choose **This date only**, **All dates**, or **This date and all following dates**. You can change your answer at any time. Choosing **No** doesn't remove the meeting from your list. Use **All meetings** or **Not going** to find it. See [Join a Meeting](../join-meeting/).

## Can I remove myself from a meeting?

Yes, if you're registered for an upcoming meeting. Select **Remove Myself** on the meeting's card or join page. You need to be signed in. If you were added through a group or mailing list, you stay a member of it. See [Join a Meeting](../join-meeting/).

## Where can I find a meeting's recording, transcript, or AI summary?

On the meeting's join page and its **Past** details page, once the meeting has ended and if the organizer turned those features on. The organizer's **Who can access recordings & summaries** setting decides who can use them: **Meeting Hosts Only**, **Meeting Guests**, or **Public**. You need to be signed in and able to open the meeting to use them. See [Past Meetings](../past-meetings/) for who can see them.

## Why can't I see the AI summary?

The organizer may have turned on **Requires host approval before sharing with guests**. Until an organizer approves the summary, only organizers can see it. See [Past Meetings](../past-meetings/).

## Where can I find meeting materials?

On the meeting's join page, in the **Meeting Materials** panel, and on the meeting's card in the Meetings list. You need to be **signed in** and have access to the meeting. For a public meeting with no join restriction, any signed-in user can see them. For a private or restricted meeting, only invited guests and organizers can. After the meeting runs, people who attended can see them too. Organizers can add materials to a single occurrence of a recurring meeting, or to all of them. See [Meeting Materials](../meeting-materials/).

## Where do I find the Zoom host key?

Organizers see the key on the meeting's card (**Host Key** button) and on its join page (**Host controls**). Meeting Hosts who aren't organizers see it on the join page. It's available from 70 minutes before the meeting starts until 40 minutes after it ends. In Zoom, open **Participants → Claim Host** and enter the key. See [Host Controls and the Host Key](../host-controls/).

## Where can I see who attended a past meeting?

Open the **Past** tab in Meetings and select **See Meeting Details** on the meeting. Organizers get a details page with the attendance rate and a **Participants** table. Everyone else is taken to the meeting's join page. Organizers, and people who were invited or attended, see who was there. Anyone else sees the totals only. See [Past Meetings](../past-meetings/).

## Why don't I see Reconcile Attendance?

It's for organizers of the meeting, and it's on the past meeting's details page. See [Reconcile Attendance](../reconcile-attendance/).
