## Why don't I see all four lenses?

The lenses you see depend on your roles on Linux Foundation projects. Everyone sees **Me** and **Organization**, and almost everyone sees **Projects**. **Foundation** is for board members, executive directors, foundation admins and Linux Foundation staff. See [Who can see each lens](../#who-can-see-each-lens) for the full list. If you believe you should have access to a lens that is not visible, contact your foundation or project administrator.

## Why can't I see my organization's dashboard?

Everyone can open the **Organization** lens, but your company's pages show only if your organization has given you access. Access is granted by your organization's own Organization Lens administrators, not by the Linux Foundation. Ask them to grant you access. If you are not sure who they are, your company's open source program office (OSPO) is a good place to start.

Holding another role, such as committee member, board member or executive director, does not by itself give you access to your company's pages. See [Who can access this](../#who-can-access-this) for the messages you may see and what each one means. If you are a CLA Manager, see the [Organization FAQ](../../organization/faq/#why-can-t-i-see-my-company-in-the-organization-lens).

## Why does my dashboard show different content from a colleague's?

The dashboard adapts to your roles and the active project context. If you and your colleague have different roles or have selected different projects, your dashboards will look different.

## How do I change the project I'm viewing?

Use the project context picker in the top navigation when you are in the **Project** or **Foundation** lens. Select a different project or foundation to update the entire page.

## My lens preference isn't being remembered. What should I do?

LFX Self Serve saves your lens selection in a browser cookie that lasts 30 days. If you are using private/incognito mode or your browser blocks cookies, the preference will not persist. Try using a standard browser session.

## What are the drawers I see when I click on dashboard cards?

Drawers are sliding side panels that show additional detail — such as metrics charts or related items — without navigating away from the dashboard. Close a drawer by selecting the close icon or pressing Escape.

## How do I get access to the Foundation lens?

The Foundation lens is available to board members and executive directors of a foundation, foundation admins, and Linux Foundation staff. Contact your project administrator if you need elevated access.

## Why is the Health Metrics page not accessible to me?

The Health Metrics page (`/foundation/health-metrics`) is available only to the foundation's executive director and to Linux Foundation staff. Anyone else who opens it is taken to the Foundation overview instead.
