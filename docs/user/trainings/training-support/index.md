LFX Self Serve shows your Linux Foundation training and certification purchases, enrollments, and certifications in read-only view. The purchases and programs themselves are managed by the LF Education team, and they handle questions about them. On the **Rewards** tab you can also claim Tux Rewards incentives and redeem coupons — see [Training Rewards and Coupons](../rewards/).

## Contact the LF Education team

1. Check the LF Education FAQ at [training.linuxfoundation.org/about/faqs](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/about/faqs/).
2. If the FAQ doesn't answer your question, contact the LF Education support team through their support portal at [trainingsupport.linuxfoundation.org](https://trainingsupport.linuxfoundation.org).

Include your order ID when your question is about a purchase. You can find it on the **Transactions** tab (`/profile/transactions`).

## Questions the LF Education team handles

Contact the LF Education support team for any of these:

- **Certification exams** — scheduling or rescheduling an exam, exam check-in or proctoring problems, a remote session that disconnected, and how many attempts or retakes you have left.
- **Exam vouchers** — whether a voucher is still valid, when it expires, extending a voucher, and vouchers bought in a bundle or received through a program or partner.
- **Refunds, invoices, and receipts** — for training courses, certification exams, bundles, and subscriptions, including a charge you don't recognize.
- **Payment at checkout** — payment methods accepted on the Linux Foundation training checkout.
- **Certifications** — renewals, expiry dates, and corrections to a certificate, including the name printed on it. Changing your name in LFX Self Serve does not update a certificate that has already been issued.
- **LF Education programs and subscriptions** — for example THRIVE, CARE, and Kubestronaut benefits.
- **Credly badges** — a badge for a Linux Foundation certification that Credly hasn't issued, or that shows the wrong details. If the badge is in Credly but doesn't appear on the LFX **Badges** page, see the [Badges FAQ](../../badges/faq/).

LFX Self Serve doesn't show exam rules, attempt counts, or voucher terms. Ask the LF Education team about them rather than relying on what LFX Self Serve displays.

## Questions LFX support handles

LFX support can help with these training-related questions:

- **Tux Rewards** — reward points that haven't appeared, claiming an incentive, and redeeming or copying a Tux Rewards coupon on the **Rewards** tab. See [Training Rewards and Coupons](../rewards/).
- **Missing data in LFX Self Serve** — a training or certification purchase that still isn't on the **Transactions** tab after 48 hours, or an enrollment that still isn't on the Training & Certifications page after 24 hours.
