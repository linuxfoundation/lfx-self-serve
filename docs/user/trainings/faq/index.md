## How do I contact the LF Education team?

Check the LF Education FAQ at [training.linuxfoundation.org/about/faqs](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/about/faqs/) first. If it doesn't answer your question, contact the LF Education support team through their support portal at [trainingsupport.linuxfoundation.org](https://trainingsupport.linuxfoundation.org). See [Get Help with Training and Certifications](../training-support/) for which questions they handle.

## When does my exam voucher expire, and can I extend it?

LFX Self Serve doesn't show voucher terms or expiry dates for exam vouchers you bought or received through a program. Contact the LF Education support team at [trainingsupport.linuxfoundation.org](https://trainingsupport.linuxfoundation.org) to check a voucher or ask about an extension. For Tux Rewards coupons, see [Training Rewards and Coupons](../rewards/).

## How do I schedule or reschedule an exam, or check how many attempts I have left?

Exam scheduling, rescheduling, and retakes aren't managed in LFX Self Serve. Contact the LF Education support team at [trainingsupport.linuxfoundation.org](https://trainingsupport.linuxfoundation.org).

## How do I get a refund or invoice for a training or certification purchase?

Refunds, invoices, and receipts for training courses, certification exams, bundles, and subscriptions are handled by the LF Education team. Contact them at [trainingsupport.linuxfoundation.org](https://trainingsupport.linuxfoundation.org) and include your order ID from the **Transactions** tab (`/profile/transactions`).

## Who do I ask about THRIVE, CARE, or Kubestronaut benefits?

These are LF Education programs. Contact the LF Education support team at [trainingsupport.linuxfoundation.org](https://trainingsupport.linuxfoundation.org).

## How do I enroll in a training course?

Enrollment is handled on the Linux Foundation training platform ([training.linuxfoundation.org](https://training.linuxfoundation.org)). LFX Self Serve shows your enrolled courses in read-only view. In the **Useful links** section (right sidebar) on the Training & Certifications page, select **LF Education & Certification** to browse the training catalog and enroll.

## Why don't I see a training I enrolled in?

There may be a delay of up to 24 hours between enrolling on the training platform and the enrollment appearing in LFX Self Serve. If a course is still missing after 24 hours, contact LFX support.

A certification exam you are enrolled in appears on the **Enrolled Trainings** tab until you pass it, then on the **Certifications** tab.

## How do I see my training completion certificates?

Certificates earned through Linux Foundation training appear on the **Certifications** tab in LFX Self Serve. You can also access them directly from the LF Training Portal.

## The name on my certificate is wrong. How do I change it?

Changing your name in LFX Self Serve does not change a certificate that has already been issued. The **Download** and **Download Certificate** buttons open the certificate file from the Linux Foundation training platform, so updating your name in **Profile & Account** and downloading it again gives you the same file with the same name.

To have the name on a certificate corrected, for example if it is printed in a different alphabet or spelled wrong, contact the LF Education support team at [trainingsupport.linuxfoundation.org](https://trainingsupport.linuxfoundation.org). Include the course or exam code (for example, LFS184) and your name exactly as it should appear.

## Do training enrollments expire?

Training enrollment expiry, if applicable, is shown in the LF Training Portal. Check your course details there for any time limits.

## Can I earn a badge by completing a training course or certification?

Yes. Completing certain Linux Foundation training programs qualifies you for LFX badges. After completing your training or certification, you will receive a separate email with instructions on how to claim your digital badge at our partner Acclaim’s website.

If it has been more than 2 days since you received your Pass notification and you still have not received the email from Credly, please be sure to check your spam folder and then contact the LF Education support team in their support portal at <https://trainingsupport.linuxfoundation.org>

## How do I earn and use training reward points?

You earn reward points on eligible Linux Foundation training and certification purchases. Your **Total Reward Points** and progress toward your next reward appear on the **Rewards** tab of the Training & Certifications page. See [Training Rewards and Coupons](../rewards/) for how to claim incentives and redeem coupons, or the [Rewards FAQ](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/tux-rewards/) for how points are earned and how long they last.

## How do I redeem a training coupon?

Open the **Rewards** tab on the Training & Certifications page. In the **My Coupons** column, use **Copy Code** to copy an available coupon, or **Redeem** to redeem one using your reward points, then paste the code at checkout on the Linux Foundation training platform. See [Training Rewards and Coupons](../rewards/) for details.

## Where can I find a list of available training courses?

Visit the Linux Foundation training catalog at [https://training.linuxfoundation.org](https://training.linuxfoundation.org). You can also use the **LF Education & Certification** link in the **Useful links** section (right sidebar) of the Training & Certifications page.
