These steps apply to anyone with access to at least one company in the Company lens, such as a company admin (editor or viewer). A CLA (Contributor License Agreement) Manager or initial CLA Manager designee (the person nominated to sign the company's corporate CLA and become its first CLA Manager) with no other role on a company sees only its **EasyCLA** page, not the Company Overview. To pick the company the page shows, see [Choose and switch a company](../choose-a-company/).

## What is the Company Overview?

The Company Overview is the **Dashboard** of the Company lens: a one-page summary of how the selected company takes part in Linux Foundation (LF) foundations and projects. On a larger screen, it opens when you select **Company** in the lens switcher, and from **Dashboard** at the top of the sidebar. On a phone or small screen, selecting **Company** in the navigation menu only shows that lens's menu: select **Dashboard** in it to open the page. If your only role on that company is CLA Manager or initial CLA Manager designee, you land on its **EasyCLA** page instead. See [Where do I find EasyCLA?](../easycla/#where-do-i-find-easycla). Its address is `/company/<company>/overview`.

The page has three parts, from top to bottom:

1. A heading with the company's name followed by **Overview**, such as **Acme Overview**, and a label with the company's Linux Foundation membership tier, when it has one.
2. **Involvement** — seven cards for the company's contributions, events and training.
3. **Foundations and Projects** — four summary tiles and a table of every foundation the company is involved in.

## What do the Involvement cards show?

The Involvement cards count the selected company's active contributors, maintainer roles, event attendees and speakers, and its people's training: people with training activity, certified people and courses taken. The section heading names the company, such as **Acme's Involvement**, followed by **across N LF foundations**, or **No engagement yet** when the company is involved in no foundation or that count did not load.

Use the buttons above the cards to show only one group: **All**, **Contribution**, **Event** or **Education**. Use the arrow buttons at the right of the heading to scroll through the cards. Select one of the three **Education** cards to open [Training & Certification](../training/); the other cards are for reading only. If a card's figures did not load, it shows 0, except **Maintainer roles** and the **Education** cards, which show a dash (**—**).

| Card                              | What it counts                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Active Contributors**           | People with at least one code contribution (a commit, pull request, review or comment) in LF and non-LF projects over the past 2 years. Each person is credited to the employer they had when they did the work, so people who have since left are included. Only the company shown on the company's LFX Insights page is counted, in the repositories LFX Insights counts; if the company has no LFX Insights company, or that company has no code activity in the past two years, every related company is counted (see [Company scope](../../dashboards/#company-scope)). Bots and people marked as team members in LFX are left out. The card's subtitle reads **LF and non-LF projects, past 2 years, credited to** the company **at the time of their work**.         |
| **Maintainer roles**              | People named as maintainers in a project's governance files, counted once per project and credited to their current employer, in LF and non-LF projects over the past 2 years. People marked as team members in LFX are left out. A dash (**—**) in place of a number means the roles cannot be read right now; it does not mean zero.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Event Attendees**               | **Employees who registered or attended foundation events** — the number of distinct people who registered or attended a foundation event that has ended in the last 2 years and registered or spoke with a company email address on record, or whose LF account matches someone on the company roster (**Employee match**). People who only named the company on their registration or talk submission are not counted here; **Self-reported only** and **All** on the Events page include them. Cancelled and denied registrations are not counted. The headline counts each person once; the chart shows people per month, so the headline is not the sum of the chart's points. The same numbers, with filters, are on [Events and Event Attendees](../company-events/). |
| **Event Speakers**                | **Employee speakers accepted at foundation events** — the number of distinct people with an accepted talk at a foundation event that has ended in the last 2 years, counted under **Employee match** like Event Attendees. The headline counts each person once, so it is not the sum of the chart's monthly points.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **People with training activity** | Distinct people on the company's people list, current and former, with an enrollment or a certificate in the past 2 years, with the date of the data (such as **People · Past 2 years · as of Nov 4, 2026**). It is the same number as the card of that name on Training & Certification with its default time range and no filters, and as the **Trainees** card on People › All Employees, which counts current employees only, plus any former employees still on the people list. Learners linked to the company who are not on its people list are not counted here                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Certified people**              | Distinct people on the people list who earned a certificate in the past 2 years, with the number of certifications underneath, such as **12 certifications · Past 2 years · as of Nov 4, 2026**. The same numbers as the **Certified people** and **Certifications** cards on Training & Certification with its defaults                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Courses taken**                 | Person-course pairs on the people list in the past 2 years, a course taken more than once counting once, with the date of the data. The same number as the **Courses taken** card on Training & Certification with its defaults                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

On **Active Contributors** and **Maintainer roles**, the information icon next to the title gives the full counting rule. When the monthly figures are available, their bar charts show one bar per month for the last 12 months, and the caption under the chart names that window, for example **Per month, Nov 2025 – Oct 2026 to date, LF and non-LF projects**. The current month is counted up to today. If the monthly figures cannot be read, the chart is empty and has no caption. The number on the card is counted separately, so an empty chart does not mean the number is zero.

## What do the Foundations and Projects tiles show?

The four tiles at the top of the Foundations and Projects section total the selected company's foundation memberships, projects, governance seats and this week's meetings. **Foundations** counts only the foundations it is a member of, so it can be lower than the number of rows in the table below. The section heading names the company, such as **Acme's Foundations and Projects**. Under each total, a line breaks it down. A category with nothing to count is left off that line, and a dash (**—**) means no category has anything to count.

| Tile                   | What it counts                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Foundations**        | The foundations where the company is a member with a membership tier, broken down by that tier, such as **Gold (2), Silver (1)**. **Non-member** foundations and the **Outside LF** row are listed in the table but not counted here |
| **Projects**           | The projects the company is involved in, broken down by influence: **Leading**, **Contributing**, **Participating** and **Silent**                                                                                                   |
| **Governance Roles**   | Seats held by the company's people, broken down into **Board members** and **Committee members**                                                                                                                                     |
| **Meetings This Week** | This week's meetings in the company's foundations, broken down into **Board**, **Technical**, **Marketing**, **Working Group** and **Other**                                                                                         |

## What does the foundations table show?

The foundations table lists one row per foundation the selected company is involved in, with the company's role in each, plus an **Outside LF** row for its projects that are not part of a Linux Foundation foundation. Each row shows the foundation's logo and name, how many projects the company is involved in there (such as **3 projects involved**), and a membership label: the tier followed by **Member** (such as **Gold Member**), **Non-member**, or **Outside LF**.

| Column                       | What it shows                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Foundation**               | The foundation's name, logo, project count and membership label                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Company Role**             | **Director**, **Member** or **Non-Member**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Voting Status**            | **Voting** or **Observer**. A dash (**—**) means there is no voting status to show                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Governance Participation** | How often the company's people attend governance, working group and project meetings: **Active** above 66% attendance, **Partial** from 33% to 66%, and **Inactive** below 33%. Hover over the label to see the attendance percentage, rounded to a whole number. A dash (**—**) means there is no attendance to measure |

### How do I see the projects inside a foundation?

Select the arrow at the start of a foundation's row to expand it. The expanded row lists **Projects Involved (N)**, with one line per project:

| Column                       | What it shows                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Project**                  | The project's name, with a colored dot for the company's influence: dark green for **Leading**, blue for **Contributing**, amber for **Participating** and gray for **Silent**                                                                                                             |
| **LF maintainer roles**      | Maintainer roles the company's people hold on the project over the past 2 years, with people marked as team members left out. Only LF projects are counted. A dash (**—**) means the project is not an LF project, or its maintainer roles cannot be read right now; it does not mean zero |
| **Contributors**             | The company's people who contributed to the project                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Collaborators**            | The company's people who collaborated on the project                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Commits (last 12 months)** | Commits the company's people authored in the last 12 months, on any branch, including pull-request commits not yet merged. Each commit is counted once, and bots are left out                                                                                                              |

If the company has no active projects in that foundation, the expanded row says **No projects from this foundation are currently active for your org.**

## Where do the links on the Company Overview go?

The Company Overview links to the **Memberships**, **Projects** and **Training & Certification** pages of the same company. The other Involvement cards and the summary tiles do not link anywhere.

1. **View all on Memberships page ›**, at the right of the Foundations and Projects heading, opens the **Memberships** page.
2. Selecting a foundation's row opens the **Memberships** page. The **Outside LF** row does not open anything.
3. Selecting a project inside an expanded foundation opens that project's page under **Projects**. Only LF projects open a page; other projects are listed for reference. See [What is a project detail page?](../projects/#what-is-a-project-detail-page).
4. Selecting one of the three **Education** cards, **People with training activity**, **Certified people** or **Courses taken**, opens **Training & Certification** with its defaults.

On a keyboard, move to a foundation or project row and press Enter or Space to open it.

## What do the messages on the Company Overview mean?

The Company Overview shows a message in place of the page or a section when there is nothing to show, you cannot open a company, or something did not load.

| Message                                        | What it means and what to do                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **No company selected**                        | LFX found no company you can open. Double-check your invite status with your admin, or select **contact support**.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **No company linked to your account**          | LFX did not find a company you have access to, and could not confirm that your employer is a Linux Foundation member. **Add an affiliation** links your profile to your employer, but an affiliation does not by itself give you access: you still need a grant from your company's Company Lens administrators. If you already added one, select **Already added one? Contact support** |
| **You do not have access to the Company Lens** | Your employer is a Linux Foundation member, but you have not been granted access to it. Access is limited to a small set of admins: ask one, likely your open source program office (OSPO), to grant it, or select **Contact support**                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Some companies could not be loaded**         | Your companies did not load. This is a temporary problem and does not change your access. Select **Retry**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **No foundations or projects yet.**            | The company is not involved in any foundation or project yet, so the table is empty                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **No engagement yet**                          | Shown next to the Involvement heading. The company is involved in no foundation, or the count of its foundations did not load. If you expected foundations, reload the page                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Couldn't load Foundations and Projects.**    | The Foundations and Projects section did not load. Select **Retry**. The Involvement cards above it are not affected                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

If a link shows a message such as **You do not have access to this company** in place of the dashboard, the link did not open a company. The company may not exist, you may not have access to it, or the lookup may have failed for a moment. See [What happens when a link does not open a company?](../choose-a-company/#what-happens-when-a-link-does-not-open-a-company).
