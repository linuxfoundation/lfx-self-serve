## How do I switch to a different company?

You switch companies from the company switcher at the top of the sidebar in the Company Lens. Select it, then select another company from the list. You stay on the same page, now showing the company you picked, unless your only role on that company is CLA (Contributor License Agreement) Manager or initial CLA Manager designee (the person nominated to sign the company's corporate CLA and become its first CLA Manager), in which case you land on its **EasyCLA** page. The browser's Back button usually returns you to the previous one. If you can open only one company, the switcher has no list to open. See [How do I switch to a different company?](../choose-a-company/#how-do-i-switch-to-a-different-company).

## What does the Company Overview dashboard show?

The Company Overview, the **Dashboard** of the Company Lens, summarizes how the selected company takes part in Linux Foundation foundations and projects. Its **Involvement** cards count the company's active contributors, maintainer roles, event attendees and speakers, certified employees and training enrollments. Its **Foundations and Projects** section totals the foundations it is a member of, its projects, governance roles and this week's meetings, then lists each foundation with the company's role, voting status and meeting participation. Selecting a foundation row, other than **Outside LF**, opens **Memberships**, and selecting an LF project opens that project's page. Return on investment (ROI) figures are on a separate page for Linux Foundation staff. See [What is the Company Overview?](../company-overview/#what-is-the-company-overview).

## What does the Memberships page show?

The Memberships page lists the selected company's foundation memberships. **Active** lists current memberships, **Expired** lists memberships that have ended, and **Discover** lists foundations where the company's people contribute but it has no active membership. Select a foundation name on **Active** to open that membership's key contacts, board and committee seats, and membership agreements. A CLA Manager or initial CLA Manager designee with no other role on the company sees **EasyCLA** instead of **Memberships**. See [What is the Memberships page?](../memberships/#what-is-the-memberships-page).

## What does the Projects page show?

The Projects page lists the open source projects in the selected company's workspaces, one workspace at a time. Most are projects the company is involved in, and someone who can edit the company, such as an Admin, can add others. Each row shows the project's **Health**, the company's **Technical influence** and **Ecosystem influence** (**Leading**, **Contributing**, **Participating**, or **Silent**), its **Influence Trend (1y)**, and how many of its employees are **Contributors** and **Participants** over the past 2 years. Select a project name to open its detail page, with the company's influence cards on **Our Influence** and its rank among other companies on **Leaderboards**. A CLA Manager or initial CLA Manager designee with no other role on the company sees **EasyCLA** instead of **Projects**. See [What is the Projects page?](../projects/#what-is-the-projects-page).

## How do I add a project to the Projects page?

Add it to a workspace with **Add Project**. You must be able to edit the company, for example as an Admin. In the **Company** lens, open **Projects**, pick the workspace in the workspace menu, select **Add Project**, search in **Search and select projects**, select one or more projects, then select **Add project(s)**. Workspaces are shared, so everyone who can open the company sees the change. If you can only view the company, **Add Project** is unavailable and says **You have view-only access to this company**. See [How do I add a project to a workspace?](../projects/#how-do-i-add-a-project-to-a-workspace).

## Why can't I edit this corporate CLA?

You can change a corporate CLA (CCLA) only when you are a CLA Manager named on that agreement, and a role added or removed in the last 30 minutes may not have taken effect yet. A company admin or a viewer can read every corporate CLA of the company and still be unable to edit one they are not named on. A signed corporate CLA that does not name you is marked **View only** on its card and at the top of its page. The name on the list has to match your LF username exactly, including capitalization. See [Who can see EasyCLA, and who can change a corporate CLA?](../easycla/#who-can-see-easycla-and-who-can-change-a-corporate-cla).

## Why can't I see my company in the Company Lens?

You see EasyCLA for a company you can open in the Company Lens. If you are a CLA Manager for a company that does not appear there, manage that company's agreements in the [Corporate CLA Console](https://corporate.lfcla.com). Membership does not by itself hide a company: Linux Foundation staff can open companies that are not members. A CLA Manager, or an initial CLA Manager designee, with no other Company Lens role sees **EasyCLA** and nothing else in the sidebar. See [Where do I find EasyCLA?](../easycla/#where-do-i-find-easycla).

## What does "View only" mean on a corporate CLA?

**View only** means you can read that signed corporate CLA (CCLA) but cannot change it, because you are not named on its CLA Manager list. It shows on the agreement's card in the EasyCLA list and next to its name at the top of its page, where **See who manages it** opens the **CLA Managers** tab so you can ask one of them. A corporate CLA that names you as a CLA Manager has no label. The label follows the CLA Manager list, but a role added in the last 30 minutes may not have unlocked the controls yet, so reload after waiting. See [Who can see EasyCLA, and who can change a corporate CLA?](../easycla/#who-can-see-easycla-and-who-can-change-a-corporate-cla).

## Why do I still have access after I was removed as a CLA Manager?

A CLA Manager role that was just added or just removed can take about 30 minutes before every control matches the list. Reload the page after waiting. If you removed yourself, this page updates your own controls when the list reloads, so you do not keep managing that agreement from this page while you wait. See [Who can see EasyCLA, and who can change a corporate CLA?](../easycla/#who-can-see-easycla-and-who-can-change-a-corporate-cla).

## How do I add a first CLA Manager when a corporate CLA has none?

On an agreement that is not signed yet, identify the initial CLA Manager while signing: **Start the CLA process**, then answer **Are you authorized to be a CLA Manager for your company?** On an agreement that is already signed and lists no CLA Managers, **Add CLA Manager** stays disabled, because only someone already named on it can add another. That record needs EasyCLA support. See [How do I add or remove a CLA Manager?](../easycla/#how-do-i-add-or-remove-a-cla-manager).

## Why does EasyCLA say "We couldn't load your CLAs"?

**We couldn't load your CLAs** is the title for two different failures. **You don't have an EasyCLA role at this company** means this page has nothing to show you, and **contact support** is there if you need EasyCLA assistance. **Something went wrong fetching this company's corporate CLAs** means the load failed — refresh the page. The title alone does not say which one you hit. See [Who can see EasyCLA, and who can change a corporate CLA?](../easycla/#who-can-see-easycla-and-who-can-change-a-corporate-cla).

## What does Auto Acknowledge do?

**Auto Acknowledge** is the switch on Overview. On, employees matching the Approved List are covered automatically. Off, those employees still need to individually acknowledge the CLA before they are covered. You can see the switch on any signed corporate CLA you can open. You can change it only when you are a CLA Manager named on that agreement. See [What does Auto Acknowledge do?](../easycla/#what-does-auto-acknowledge-do).

## How do I sign a corporate CLA?

You sign a corporate CLA (CCLA) from **Sign CLA** on EasyCLA, either yourself in DocuSign or by sending it to the person who should sign. Select **Sign CLA**, choose the CLA Group, and, if asked, confirm you are authorized to be a CLA Manager. **Yes** shows **Configuring CLA Manager Settings…**, then Overview offers **Proceed with signing**, which opens **Confirm Authorization to Sign the CLA**. If you answer **No** and name someone else, your part ends there and that person continues. On **Confirm Authorization to Sign the CLA**, pick one branch:

1. To sign yourself, complete **Authorization Confirmation** and **Compliance Confirmation**, select **Continue**, then **Review and Sign CCLA**. That opens DocuSign. Self Serve does not run the ceremony.
2. To have someone else sign, select **I am not authorized** without completing the confirmations. In **Identify who should sign**, enter their name and email and select **Send Signature Request Email**. See [How do I sign a corporate CLA?](../easycla/#how-do-i-sign-a-corporate-cla).

## What does a sanctioned company look like?

A sanctioned company has no status pill, and the Overview heading ends with **Unavailable**. There is no banner on the page. Signing and changes are refused. Answering the CLA Manager question while signing for that company shows **Your company cannot sign this CLA at this time. Contact support for more information.** Any other refused step shows an error when you try it. See [What does a sanctioned company look like on EasyCLA?](../easycla/#what-does-a-sanctioned-company-look-like-on-easycla).

## How do I remove someone from my company?

Remove their Company Lens access. You must be an Admin for the company. In the **Company** lens, open **People**, select the **Company Lens Access** tab, select the trash icon next to the person, then select **Remove**. This removes the access granted to them on that tab. If they can also open your company another way, for example through access to a related company, they keep that access. It does not remove them from committees, groups, mailing lists or meetings. You cannot remove the company's only Admin until another Admin is added. See [Manage Company Lens Access](../manage-company-lens-access/#remove-someone-s-access).

## How do I give someone access to my company?

Invite them from the **Company Lens Access** tab. You must be an Admin for the company. In the **Company** lens, open **People**, select the **Company Lens Access** tab, select **Add Users**, enter their email, choose **Viewer** or **Admin**, then select **Send Invite**. They appear as **Invited** until they accept. See [Manage Company Lens Access](../manage-company-lens-access/#add-someone).
