The Company lens shows one company at a time: its foundation memberships, its projects, its people and their activity, its profile, and its corporate CLAs (Contributor License Agreements). You can open a company there when you hold a role on it, and that role decides which of its pages you see.

These guides are for anyone who can open a company in the Company lens, such as a company admin or viewer, a CLA Manager, or an initial CLA Manager designee (the person nominated to sign the company's corporate CLA and become its first CLA Manager).

## What can I do in the Company lens?

You can see how the selected company takes part in Linux Foundation foundations and projects, and, with the right role, change some of it. Each sidebar page covers one part, in this order:

- **Dashboard**, the Org Overview, shows the company's involvement and its foundations and projects
- **Memberships** shows the company's foundation memberships and, for each one, its key contacts, board and committee seats, and agreements
- **Projects** shows the projects in the company's shared workspaces, its influence on each one, how it ranks against other companies, and how each company's leaderboard score breaks down
- **Crowdfunding Initiatives** shows the crowdfunding initiatives run on behalf of the company, if you are an Admin granted directly on it
- **Crowdfunding Donations** shows the crowdfunding donations made on behalf of the company
- Under **Company Engagement**:
  - **People** shows the company's employees and their board and committee seats, key contact roles, code contributions, events and training. Its **Company Lens Access** tab shows who has been granted access directly on the company and lets an Admin add, change or remove that access
  - **Code Contributions** shows the repositories the company's contributors worked in and each of their commits
  - **Events**, and **People** → **Event Attendees**, show which events the company's people registered for or attended, and let you choose how they are counted
  - **Training & Certification** shows the courses and certifications the company's people enrolled in or completed
  - **Meetings** shows how the company's employees take part in project meetings and how that adds to its influence on each project
  - **Groups** shows the working groups and committees where the company's employees hold seats, and who holds them
- **EasyCLA** lets you view the corporate CLAs the company holds and, as a CLA Manager named on one, manage it
- **Company Profile** shows the company's logo, description, website, employees, industry, sector and addresses and, if you can edit the company, lets you change its logo, description, website, Crunchbase profile, number of employees, industry and sector

In the **Foundation** lens, board members and executive directors also see the selected company's involvement in one foundation, with a detail panel behind each card. See [Company involvement in a foundation](../dashboards/company-involvement/).

Your own signed agreements, as a contributor, are on the [CLAs](../account/my-clas/) tab under Profile & Account. They are a different page.

## Who can use the Company lens?

You can open a company in the Company lens when you hold a role on it: an admin or viewer role, including one inherited from a related company, or a CLA Manager or initial CLA Manager designee role. An admin or viewer can open every page in the Company lens sidebar, and **Crowdfunding Initiatives** appears there only for an Admin granted directly on that company. A CLA Manager, or an initial CLA Manager designee, with no other role on that company sees only **EasyCLA**. To give someone an admin or viewer role, see [Manage Company Lens Access](./manage-company-lens-access/).

## How do I open the Company lens?

You open it from the lens switcher, then pick the company in the company switcher at the top of the sidebar.

1. Sign in to [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev).
2. Select **Company** in the lens switcher.
3. If you can open more than one company, select the company switcher at the top of the sidebar, then select the one you want.
4. Select a page in the sidebar. **EasyCLA** is just above **Company Profile**.

See [Choose and switch a company](./choose-a-company/) for what you land on first, switching on a phone, and what a company in a page address means.
