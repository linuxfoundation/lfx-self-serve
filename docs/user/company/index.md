The Company section covers pages in the Company Lens. These steps apply to anyone who can open a company there.

## What you can do

- Open **Dashboard**, the Company Overview, to see the company's involvement and its foundations and projects
- Open **Memberships** to see the company's foundation memberships and, for each one, its key contacts, board and committee seats, and agreements
- Open **Events**, or **People** → **Event Attendees**, to see which events the company's people registered for or attended, and choose how they are counted
- Open **Projects** to see the projects in the company's shared workspaces, its influence on each one, and how it ranks against other companies
- Open **People** to see the company's employees and their board and committee seats, key contact roles, code contributions, events and training
- Open **Code Contributions** to see the repositories the company's contributors worked in and each of their commits
- Open **Training & Certification** to see the courses and certifications the company's people enrolled in or completed
- Open **Meetings** to see how the company's employees take part in project meetings and how that adds to its influence on each project
- Open **Groups** to see the working groups and committees where the company's employees hold seats, and who holds them
- Open **People** → **Company Lens Access** to see who can open the company and, as an Admin, add, change or remove their access
- Open **EasyCLA** to view and manage the corporate CLAs (Contributor License Agreements) that company holds
- Open **Crowdfunding Initiatives** to see the crowdfunding initiatives run on behalf of the company, if you have a writer role on it
- Open **Crowdfunding Donations** to see the crowdfunding donations made on behalf of the company

Your own signed agreements, as a contributor, are on the [CLAs](../account/my-clas/) tab under Profile & Account. They are a different page.

## Who this applies to

Anyone who can open a company in the Company Lens. EasyCLA is also the only Company Lens page a CLA Manager, or an initial CLA Manager designee (the person nominated to sign the company's corporate CLA and become its first CLA Manager), sees when they have no other role on that company.

## Navigation

Go to **app.lfx.dev**, switch to the **Company** lens, and select the company. EasyCLA is in the sidebar, just above **Company Profile**.
