## Who can create a survey?

People who can manage surveys for the project or foundation, or who are a writer of the group being surveyed. If you don't see **Create Survey**, contact your project administrator. Anyone who receives a survey can respond to it. See [Create a Survey](../create-survey/).

## Can I write my own questions?

No. Choose a template under **NPS Survey Template** and the questions come from it. You write the email that introduces the survey. See [Create a Survey](../create-survey/).

## What is NPS and how does it work?

Net Promoter Score (NPS) measures how likely respondents are to recommend your project on a scale of 0 to 10. Respondents scoring 9 to 10 are "promoters", 7 to 8 are "passives", and 0 to 6 are "detractors". The NPS is the percentage of promoters minus the percentage of detractors, so it ranges from -100 to +100.

## Who receives the survey?

The members of the group the survey is created for. A survey is currently created for the first group you select, so create one survey per group you want to reach. The form notes that for Board groups, only voting representatives receive it. The **Review & Confirm** step lists the target groups before you send. The number of people isn't shown there, so check the group carefully.

## Can I send a survey later?

Yes. In **Timing & Reminders**, choose **Scheduled** and pick a **Schedule Date**. The survey shows **Scheduled** until it goes out. See [Create a Survey](../create-survey/).

## How do reminders work?

When you create a survey, choose **Automatic** reminders and a frequency of every 3, 7, or 14 days. People who haven't responded are reminded until the survey closes. **Manual** removes the frequency choice, but the form doesn't confirm that reminders are switched off, so don't rely on it to prevent them.

## How do respondents access a survey?

They receive an email with a **Take Survey** button. The survey also appears on their own **Surveys** page, where they can select **Take Survey**. See [Respond to a Survey](../respond-to-a-survey/).

## Can I see individual responses?

Yes, if you can manage the survey. Open the survey's **Results** and select the **Responses** tab. You see each recipient's name, organization, delivery status, score, and comment, and you can filter and search. If you are a group writer but not a project writer, open the survey from the group's **Surveys** tab. See [View Survey Results](../survey-results/).

## Can I see results while the survey is open?

Yes. The **Overview** tab shows participation, the NPS, and comments collected so far. The panel doesn't refresh itself, so reload the page and reopen **Results** to see new responses. The final results are the same view once the survey closes.

## Can I edit or close a survey after it is sent?

No. Once a survey has been sent, you can't change its audience, email, or dates, and you can't close it early. A survey closes by itself on its cutoff date. If something needs to change, create a new survey. You may see **Close Survey**, **Duplicate Survey**, or **Save as Draft** in the app, and **Delete** on a draft, but these don't take effect yet. Don't use **Edit** on a draft: it opens a form with default values instead of the saved draft, and saving it sends a new survey straight away, with no confirmation.

## Can I change my answer after I respond?

While the survey is open, select **Update** on your **Surveys** page, or **Update your response** in the response panel. This opens the survey again. Once it closes, you can only view your response.

## How do I see which surveys are still open?

Use the **Open** and **Closed** tabs on the **Surveys** page. Draft and scheduled surveys appear under **All**.

## Why don't I see any surveys?

- On a project or foundation, make sure you have the right one selected in the project picker. Surveys are scoped to a project.
- In your own view (**Me**), you only see surveys you were invited to.
- If you can't see **Results** or **Create Survey**, you may not have permission to manage surveys there. Group writers can open results from their group's **Surveys** tab.

## Why didn't someone get the survey email?

In **Results**, open the **Responses** tab and filter **Delivery Status** to **Failed** or **Pending**. Those invitations didn't reach the person, so follow up with them another way. Select **Load more responses** until the whole list is loaded first, because the filter only covers the responses already loaded. See [View Survey Results](../survey-results/).

## What is the difference between Surveys and Votes?

**Surveys** collect feedback and opinions. **Votes** are formal decision-making polls used in governance, such as committee elections or policy decisions. See [Votes](../../votes/).
