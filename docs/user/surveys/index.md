Surveys let you collect structured feedback from the members of your groups. In LFX Self Serve, a survey is an NPS (Net Promoter Score) survey sent by email. NPS measures how likely people are to recommend your project or community on a scale of 0 to 10.

## What you can do

If you manage surveys for a project or foundation:

- [Create a survey](create-survey/) and send it now or schedule it, with reminders and your own email
- [Find and read your surveys](manage-surveys/) by status, group, and type
- [See results](survey-results/): participation, the NPS, comments, and each person's response

If you were invited to a survey:

- [Respond to a survey](respond-to-a-survey/), then check or update your answer

## Where to find Surveys

Select **Surveys** in the sidebar. What you see depends on the view you are in:

- **Project or foundation view.** The surveys sent for that project or foundation. If you can manage surveys, you also see **Create Survey**.
- **Me view.** The surveys you have been invited to, across all foundations and projects.
- **A group's Surveys tab.** The surveys sent to that group, with **Create Survey** for people who can manage it.

Creating a survey requires permission to manage surveys for the project, or to be a writer of the group. Seeing individual responses on the **Surveys** page requires project permission, and group writers see them from the group's **Surveys** tab. If you don't see **Create Survey** or **Results**, contact your project administrator.

## Key concepts

- **Net Promoter Score (NPS).** The percentage of promoters (scores of 9 to 10) minus the percentage of detractors (0 to 6). Passives (7 to 8) count toward the total but not the score.
- **Survey template.** The questions come from the template you choose. You write the email that goes with it.
- **Group.** A survey is sent to the members of a group. A survey is currently created for one group, so create one survey per group you want to reach. For Board groups, the form notes that it goes only to voting representatives.
- **Cutoff date.** The date a survey closes. It closes by itself on that date.
