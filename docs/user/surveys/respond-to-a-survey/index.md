If you belong to a group that a survey was sent to, you receive it by email and it also appears on your own **Surveys** page. You don't need any special permission to respond.

## Take a survey

You can start from either place:

- **From the email.** Select the **Take Survey** button in the invitation.
- **From LFX Self Serve.** Sign in to [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev), switch to your own view (**Me**), and select **Surveys** in the sidebar. Find the survey and select **Take Survey**.

The survey opens in a new browser tab on the survey provider's site. Answer it there. You don't need to come back to LFX Self Serve afterward.

## Your Surveys page

Your **Surveys** page lists the surveys you have been invited to across all foundations and projects. For each survey you see its name, **Survey Type**, **Group**, **Project**, when it is **Due**, and its **Status**.

- **Open** (shown with a warning color): you haven't responded yet.
- **Submitted**: you responded, and the survey is still open.
- **Closed**: the survey has ended.

Under **Due**, open surveys also show a countdown such as "Closes in 5 days", "Closes tomorrow", or "Closes today".

The tabs above the list (**All**, **Open**, and **Closed**) show how many surveys are in each. You can search by survey name and filter by group and survey type. If you have a board or project role, you can also filter by foundation and project.

If you don't see any surveys, you see "No surveys yet. Surveys you've been invited to complete will appear here."

## What you can do on each row

- **Take Survey**: on an open survey you haven't responded to. Opens the survey.
- **Update**: on an open survey you already responded to. Opens the survey so you can change your answer until it closes.
- **View**: on a closed survey. Opens your response.

You can also select the survey name, or anywhere on the row, to open **Your response** panel.

## Your response panel

The panel on the right shows where you stand:

- **If you responded to an NPS survey:** your score, with "on a scale of 0–10", and when you submitted it. If the survey is still open, **Update your response** opens the survey again.
- **If you haven't responded and the survey is open:** "You haven't responded yet", with a **Take the survey** link.
- **If the survey hasn't gone out yet:** "This survey hasn't been sent yet. You'll receive an invitation once the survey opens."
- **If the survey closed and you didn't respond:** "You didn't respond to this survey. You were invited but didn't submit a response before the survey closed."
- **If your answer is on file but the details aren't shown yet:** "Your response is on file but the detailed answers aren't available yet. Try again later or use the survey link to review your submission."
