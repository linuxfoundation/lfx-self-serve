The Organization section covers pages in the Organization lens. These steps apply to anyone who can open an organization there.

## What you can do

- Open **Dashboard**, the Org Overview, to see the organization's involvement and its foundations and projects
- Open **Projects** to see the projects the organization is involved in, its influence on each one, and how it ranks against other organizations
- Open **People** → **Org Lens Access** to see who can open the organization and, as an Admin, add, change or remove their access
- Open **EasyCLA** to view and manage the corporate CLAs (Contributor License Agreements) that organization holds

Your own signed agreements, as a contributor, are on the [CLAs](../account/my-clas/) tab under Profile & Account. They are a different page.

## Who this applies to

Anyone who can open an organization in the Organization lens. EasyCLA is also the only Organization lens page a CLA Manager, or an initial CLA Manager designee (the person nominated to sign the company's corporate CLA and become its first CLA Manager), sees when they have no other role on that organization.

## Navigation

Go to **app.lfx.dev**, switch to the **Organization** lens, and select the organization. EasyCLA is in the sidebar, just above **Organization Profile**.
