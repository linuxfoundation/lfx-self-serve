The Organization lens shows one organization at a time: its foundation memberships, its projects, its people and their activity, its profile, and its corporate CLAs (Contributor License Agreements). You can open an organization there when you hold a role on it, and that role decides which of its pages you see.

These guides are for anyone who can open an organization in the Organization lens, such as an organization admin or viewer, a CLA Manager, or an initial CLA Manager designee (the person nominated to sign the company's corporate CLA and become its first CLA Manager).

## What can I do in the Organization lens?

You can see how the selected organization takes part in Linux Foundation foundations and projects, and, with the right role, change some of it. Each sidebar page covers one part, in this order:

- **Dashboard**, the Org Overview, shows the organization's involvement and its foundations and projects
- **Memberships** shows the organization's foundation memberships and, for each one, its key contacts, board and committee seats, and agreements
- **Projects** shows the projects in the organization's shared workspaces, its influence on each one, how it ranks against other organizations, and how each organization's leaderboard score breaks down
- **Crowdfunding Initiatives** shows the crowdfunding initiatives run on behalf of the organization, if you are an Admin granted directly on it
- **Crowdfunding Donations** shows the crowdfunding donations made on behalf of the organization
- Under **Organization Engagement**:
  - **People** shows the organization's employees and their board and committee seats, key contact roles, code contributions, events and training. Its **Org Lens Access** tab shows who can open the organization and lets an Admin add, change or remove their access
  - **Code Contributions** shows the repositories the organization's contributors worked in and each of their commits
  - **Events**, and **People** → **Event Attendees**, show which events the organization's people registered for or attended, and let you choose how they are counted
  - **Training & Certification** shows the courses and certifications the organization's people enrolled in or completed
  - **Meetings** shows how the organization's employees take part in project meetings and how that adds to its influence on each project
  - **Groups** shows the working groups and committees where the organization's employees hold seats, and who holds them
- **EasyCLA** lets you view the corporate CLAs the organization holds and, as a CLA Manager named on one, manage it
- **Organization Profile** shows the organization's logo, description, website, employees, industry, sector and addresses and, if you can edit the organization, lets you change its logo, description, website, Crunchbase profile, number of employees, industry and sector

In the **Foundation** lens, board members and executive directors also see the selected organization's involvement in one foundation, with a detail panel behind each card. See [Organization involvement in a foundation](../dashboards/organization-involvement/).

Your own signed agreements, as a contributor, are on the [CLAs](../account/my-clas/) tab under Profile & Account. They are a different page.

## Who can use the Organization lens?

You can open an organization in the Organization lens when you hold a role on it: an admin or viewer role, or a CLA Manager or initial CLA Manager designee role. An admin or viewer can open every page in the Organization lens sidebar, and **Crowdfunding Initiatives** appears there only for an Admin granted directly on that organization. A CLA Manager, or an initial CLA Manager designee, with no other role on that organization sees only **EasyCLA**. To give someone an admin or viewer role, see [Manage Org Lens Access](./manage-org-lens-access/).

## How do I open the Organization lens?

You open it from the lens switcher, then pick the organization in the organization switcher at the top of the sidebar.

1. Sign in to [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev).
2. Select **Organization** in the lens switcher.
3. If you can open more than one organization, select the organization switcher at the top of the sidebar, then select the one you want.
4. Select a page in the sidebar. **EasyCLA** is just above **Organization Profile**.

See [Choose and switch an organization](./choose-an-organization/) for what you land on first, switching on a phone, and what an organization in a page address means.
