The Organization section covers pages in the Organization lens. These steps apply to anyone who can open an organization there.

## What you can do

- Open **Dashboard**, the Org Overview, to see the organization's involvement and its foundations and projects
- Open **Memberships** to see the organization's foundation memberships and, for each one, its key contacts, board and committee seats, and agreements
- Open **Events**, or **People** → **Event Attendees**, to see which events the organization's people registered for or attended, and choose how they are counted
- Open **Projects** to see the projects in the organization's shared workspaces, its influence on each one, how it ranks against other organizations, and how each organization's leaderboard score breaks down
- Open **People** to see the organization's employees and their board and committee seats, key contact roles, code contributions, events and training
- Open **Code Contributions** to see the repositories the organization's contributors worked in and each of their commits
- Open **Training & Certification** to see the courses and certifications the organization's people enrolled in or completed
- Open **Meetings** to see how the organization's employees take part in project meetings and how that adds to its influence on each project
- Open **Groups** to see the working groups and committees where the organization's employees hold seats, and who holds them
- Open **Organization Profile** to see the organization's logo, description, website, employees, industry, sector and addresses and, if you can edit the organization, change its logo, description, website, Crunchbase profile, number of employees, industry and sector
- Open **People** → **Org Lens Access** to see who can open the organization and, as an Admin, add, change or remove their access
- Open **EasyCLA** to view and manage the corporate CLAs (Contributor License Agreements) that organization holds
- Open **Crowdfunding Initiatives** to see the crowdfunding initiatives run on behalf of the organization, if you are an Admin granted directly on it
- Open **Crowdfunding Donations** to see the crowdfunding donations made on behalf of the organization

In the **Foundation** lens, board members and executive directors also see the selected organization's involvement in one foundation, with a detail panel behind each card. See [Organization involvement in a foundation](../dashboards/organization-involvement/).

Your own signed agreements, as a contributor, are on the [CLAs](../account/my-clas/) tab under Profile & Account. They are a different page.

## Who this applies to

Anyone who can open an organization in the Organization lens. EasyCLA is also the only Organization lens page a CLA Manager, or an initial CLA Manager designee (the person nominated to sign the company's corporate CLA and become its first CLA Manager), sees when they have no other role on that organization.

## Navigation

Go to **app.lfx.dev**, switch to the **Organization** lens, and select the organization. EasyCLA is in the sidebar, just above **Organization Profile**.
