These steps apply to anyone with access to at least one organization in the Organization lens, such as an organization admin (editor or viewer). A CLA (Contributor License Agreement) Manager or initial CLA Manager designee (the person nominated to sign the company's corporate CLA and become its first CLA Manager) with no other role on an organization sees only its **EasyCLA** page, not the Org Overview. To pick the organization the page shows, see [Choose and switch an organization](../choose-an-organization/).

## What is the Org Overview?

The Org Overview is the **Dashboard** of the Organization lens: a one-page summary of how the selected organization takes part in Linux Foundation (LF) foundations and projects. On a larger screen, it opens when you select **Organization** in the lens switcher, and from **Dashboard** at the top of the sidebar. On a phone or small screen, selecting **Organization** in the navigation menu only shows that lens's menu: select **Dashboard** in it to open the page. If your only role on that organization is CLA Manager or initial CLA Manager designee, you land on its **EasyCLA** page instead. See [Where do I find EasyCLA?](../easycla/#where-do-i-find-easycla). Its address is `/org/<organization>/overview`.

The page has three parts, from top to bottom:

1. A heading with the organization's name followed by **Overview**, such as **Acme Overview**, and a label with the organization's Linux Foundation membership tier, when it has one.
2. **Involvement** — seven cards for the organization's contributions, events and training.
3. **Foundations and Projects** — four summary tiles and a table of every foundation the organization is involved in.

## What do the Involvement cards show?

The Involvement cards count the selected organization's active contributors, maintainer roles, event attendees and speakers, and its people's training: people with training activity, certified people and courses taken. The section heading names the organization, such as **Acme's Involvement**, followed by **across N LF foundations**, or **No engagement yet** when the organization is involved in no foundation or that count did not load.

Use the buttons above the cards to show only one group: **All**, **Contribution**, **Event** or **Education**. Use the arrow buttons at the right of the heading to scroll through the cards. Select one of the three **Education** cards to open [Training & Certification](../training/); the other cards are for reading only. If a card's figures did not load, it shows 0, except **Maintainer roles** and the **Education** cards, which show a dash (**—**).

| Card                              | What it counts                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Active Contributors**           | People with at least one code contribution (a commit, pull request, review or comment) in LF and non-LF projects over the past 2 years. Each person is credited to the employer they had when they did the work, so people who have since left are included. Only the organization shown on the company's LFX Insights page is counted, in the repositories LFX Insights counts; if the company has no LFX Insights organization, or that organization has no code activity in the past two years, every related organization is counted (see [Organization scope](../../dashboards/#organization-scope)). Bots and people marked as team members in LFX are left out. The card's subtitle reads **LF and non-LF projects, past 2 years, credited to** the organization **at the time of their work**. |
| **Maintainer roles**              | People named as maintainers in a project's governance files, counted once per project and credited to their current employer, in LF and non-LF projects over the past 2 years. People marked as team members in LFX are left out. A dash (**—**) in place of a number means the roles cannot be read right now; it does not mean zero.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Event Attendees**               | **Employees who registered or attended foundation events** — the number of distinct people who registered or attended a foundation event that has ended in the last 2 years and registered or spoke with a company email address on record, or whose LF account matches someone on the company roster (**Employee match**). People who only named the organization on their registration or talk submission are not counted here; **Self-reported only** and **All** on the Events page include them. Cancelled and denied registrations are not counted. The headline counts each person once; the chart shows people per month, so the headline is not the sum of the chart's points. The same numbers, with filters, are on [Events and Event Attendees](../org-events/).                           |
| **Event Speakers**                | **Employee speakers accepted at foundation events** — the number of distinct people with an accepted talk at a foundation event that has ended in the last 2 years, counted under **Employee match** like Event Attendees. The headline counts each person once, so it is not the sum of the chart's monthly points.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **People with training activity** | Distinct people on the organization's people list, current and former, with an enrollment or a certificate in the past 2 years, with the date of the data (such as **People · Past 2 years · as of Nov 4, 2026**). It is the same number as the card of that name on Training & Certification with its default time range and no filters, and as the **Trainees** card on People › All Employees, which counts current employees only, plus any former employees still on the people list. Learners linked to the organization who are not on its people list are not counted here                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Certified people**              | Distinct people on the people list who earned a certificate in the past 2 years, with the number of certifications underneath, such as **12 certifications · Past 2 years · as of Nov 4, 2026**. The same numbers as the **Certified people** and **Certifications** cards on Training & Certification with its defaults                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Courses taken**                 | Person-course pairs on the people list in the past 2 years, a course taken more than once counting once, with the date of the data. The same number as the **Courses taken** card on Training & Certification with its defaults                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

On **Active Contributors** and **Maintainer roles**, the information icon next to the title gives the full counting rule. When the monthly figures are available, their bar charts show one bar per month for the last 12 months, and the caption under the chart names that window, for example **Per month, Nov 2025 – Oct 2026 to date, LF and non-LF projects**. The current month is counted up to today. If the monthly figures cannot be read, the chart is empty and has no caption. The number on the card is counted separately, so an empty chart does not mean the number is zero.

## What do the Foundations and Projects tiles show?

The four tiles at the top of the Foundations and Projects section total the selected organization's foundation memberships, projects, governance seats and this week's meetings. **Foundations** counts only the foundations it is a member of, so it can be lower than the number of rows in the table below. The section heading names the organization, such as **Acme's Foundations and Projects**. Under each total, a line breaks it down. A category with nothing to count is left off that line, and a dash (**—**) means no category has anything to count.

| Tile                   | What it counts                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Foundations**        | The foundations where the organization is a member with a membership tier, broken down by that tier, such as **Gold (2), Silver (1)**. **Non-member** foundations and the **Outside LF** row are listed in the table but not counted here |
| **Projects**           | The projects the organization is involved in, broken down by influence: **Leading**, **Contributing**, **Participating** and **Silent**                                                                                                   |
| **Governance Roles**   | Seats held by the organization's people, broken down into **Board members** and **Committee members**                                                                                                                                     |
| **Meetings This Week** | This week's meetings in the organization's foundations, broken down into **Board**, **Technical**, **Marketing**, **Working Group** and **Other**                                                                                         |

## What does the foundations table show?

The foundations table lists one row per foundation the selected organization is involved in, with the organization's role in each, plus an **Outside LF** row for its projects that are not part of a Linux Foundation foundation. Each row shows the foundation's logo and name, how many projects the organization is involved in there (such as **3 projects involved**), and a membership label: the tier followed by **Member** (such as **Gold Member**), **Non-member**, or **Outside LF**.

| Column                       | What it shows                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Foundation**               | The foundation's name, logo, project count and membership label                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Org Role**                 | **Director**, **Member** or **Non-Member**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Voting Status**            | **Voting** or **Observer**. A dash (**—**) means there is no voting status to show                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Governance Participation** | How often the organization's people attend governance, working group and project meetings: **Active** above 66% attendance, **Partial** from 33% to 66%, and **Inactive** below 33%. Hover over the label to see the attendance percentage, rounded to a whole number. A dash (**—**) means there is no attendance to measure |

### How do I see the projects inside a foundation?

Select the arrow at the start of a foundation's row to expand it. The expanded row lists **Projects Involved (N)**, with one line per project:

| Column                       | What it shows                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Project**                  | The project's name, with a colored dot for the organization's influence: dark green for **Leading**, blue for **Contributing**, amber for **Participating** and gray for **Silent**                                                                                                             |
| **LF maintainer roles**      | Maintainer roles the organization's people hold on the project over the past 2 years, with people marked as team members left out. Only LF projects are counted. A dash (**—**) means the project is not an LF project, or its maintainer roles cannot be read right now; it does not mean zero |
| **Contributors**             | The organization's people who contributed to the project                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Collaborators**            | The organization's people who collaborated on the project                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Commits (last 12 months)** | Commits the organization's people authored in the last 12 months, on any branch, including pull-request commits not yet merged. Each commit is counted once, and bots are left out                                                                                                              |

If the organization has no active projects in that foundation, the expanded row says **No projects from this foundation are currently active for your org.**

## Where do the links on the Org Overview go?

The Org Overview links to the **Memberships**, **Projects** and **Training & Certification** pages of the same organization. The other Involvement cards and the summary tiles do not link anywhere.

1. **View all on Memberships page ›**, at the right of the Foundations and Projects heading, opens the **Memberships** page.
2. Selecting a foundation's row opens the **Memberships** page. The **Outside LF** row does not open anything.
3. Selecting a project inside an expanded foundation opens that project's page under **Projects**. Only LF projects open a page; other projects are listed for reference. See [What is a project detail page?](../projects/#what-is-a-project-detail-page).
4. Selecting one of the three **Education** cards, **People with training activity**, **Certified people** or **Courses taken**, opens **Training & Certification** with its defaults.

On a keyboard, move to a foundation or project row and press Enter or Space to open it.

## What do the messages on the Org Overview mean?

The Org Overview shows a message in place of the page or a section when there is nothing to show, you cannot open an organization, or something did not load.

| Message                                             | What it means and what to do                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **No organization selected**                        | LFX found no organization you can open. Double-check your invite status with your admin, or select **contact support**.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **No organization linked to your account**          | LFX did not find an organization you have access to, and could not confirm that your employer is a Linux Foundation member. **Add an affiliation** links your profile to your employer, but an affiliation does not by itself give you access: you still need a grant from your organization's Organization Lens administrators. If you already added one, select **Already added one? Contact support** |
| **You do not have access to the Organization Lens** | Your employer is a Linux Foundation member, but you have not been granted access to it. Access is limited to a small set of admins: ask one, likely your open source program office (OSPO), to grant it, or select **Contact support**                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Some organizations could not be loaded**          | Your organizations did not load. This is a temporary problem and does not change your access. Select **Retry**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **No foundations or projects yet.**                 | The organization is not involved in any foundation or project yet, so the table is empty                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **No engagement yet**                               | Shown next to the Involvement heading. The organization is involved in no foundation, or the count of its foundations did not load. If you expected foundations, reload the page                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Couldn't load Foundations and Projects.**         | The Foundations and Projects section did not load. Select **Retry**. The Involvement cards above it are not affected                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

If a link shows a message such as **You do not have access to this organization** in place of the dashboard, the link did not open an organization. The organization may not exist, you may not have access to it, or the lookup may have failed for a moment. See [What happens when a link does not open an organization?](../choose-an-organization/#what-happens-when-a-link-does-not-open-an-organization).
