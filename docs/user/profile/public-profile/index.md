Your public profile is a page that showcases your contributions, credentials, and activity across the Linux Foundation to anyone — no sign-in required. It is separate from the private Profile section you use to manage your account.

Your public profile is only visible when you set it to public. By default it is private. See [Public profile settings](../public-profile-settings/) to make it public and choose what appears.

## Your public profile URL

When your profile is public, it is available at:

```text
app.lfx.dev/u/<username>
```

`<username>` is the username shown in the Edit Profile drawer. You can copy the exact link from [Public profile settings](../public-profile-settings/) once your profile is public.

## What visitors can see

Which sections appear depends on your [public profile settings](../public-profile-settings/) and on whether you have data for that section. When enabled, a visitor may see:

- **Header** — your name, job title, affiliation, avatar, and a short set of social links.
- **About** — your long-form **About Me** bio.
- **Project contributions** — your commits, pull requests, and issues across projects.
- **Badges** — credentialing badges you have earned across projects.
- **Certifications** — certifications you have earned, with dates.
- **Trainings** — training programs you have enrolled in or completed.

You control each of these sections individually. If you turn a section off, or have no data for it, it does not appear.

## When your profile is private

If your profile is set to private, a visitor who opens your `/u/<username>` link sees a **Private Profile** message instead of any profile content — none of your sections, contributions, or personal details are shown. The page confirms that the username belongs to a private profile; it simply withholds the content.

A link to a username that does not exist shows a different **Profile Not Found** message, so the two cases are distinguishable.

## Keeping your public profile up to date

Changes you make in [Public profile settings](../public-profile-settings/) take effect the next time your public profile is regenerated, so a change may not appear instantly. The details themselves — name, About Me, job title, and so on — come from the fields you set in the [Edit Profile drawer](../edit-profile/).
