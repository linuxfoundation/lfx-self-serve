## Where do I edit my profile details?

Open [**Profile & Account**](/profile) and select **Edit profile** in the profile panel on the right. A drawer opens where you can update your About Me, name, photo, job title, company, primary email, location, and t-shirt size. Select **Save Changes** when done. Two of these are exceptions that save immediately when you make them — uploading a **photo** and choosing a **primary email** — so **Cancel** will not revert those two. See [Edit your profile](../edit-profile/).

## What is my LFID, and where do I find it?

Your LFID (Linux Foundation ID) is your LFX **username**. It is not your email address. To find it, sign in to [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev), then use any of these:

- Open your account menu in the sidebar. Your LFID appears under your name and email.
- Open [**Profile & Account**](/profile). Your LFID appears under your name in the profile panel, labelled **LFID**, with a button to copy it.
- In the profile panel, select **Edit profile**. Your LFID is the **Username (read-only)** field.

You can't change your username. It is also the `<username>` in your public profile address, `app.lfx.dev/u/<username>`. See [Edit your profile](../edit-profile/#find-your-lfid-username).

## How do I update my profile photo?

Open the **Edit profile** drawer and select the pencil button on your avatar to upload a new image. Accepted formats are **PNG, JPEG, or WEBP**, up to **2 MB**.

## Is there a limit on my About Me / bio?

Yes — **About Me** can hold up to **2,000 characters**. A live counter under the field shows how many you have used, and typing or pasting beyond the limit is ignored. A simple emoji counts as one character, though a few elaborate emoji (such as flags or combined "family" glyphs) count as more than one.

## How do I make my profile public and control what it shows?

Your profile is private by default. Open [**Profile & Account**](/profile), select **Public profile settings** in the profile panel, and switch **Profile visibility** to **Public**. From there you can toggle which sections appear (contributions, badges, certifications, About Me, and more). Changes save automatically. See [Public profile settings](../public-profile-settings/) and [Your public profile](../public-profile/).

## Do I need a public profile to see my own contributions?

No. Your contributions appear under **Project Involvement** on the **Work history & Affiliations** tab (`/profile/attributions`) whether your profile is public or private. **Profile visibility** and the section toggles in **Public profile settings** only control what visitors see at `app.lfx.dev/u/<username>`. If your contributions are missing there too, see [Why don't my GitHub contributions show up?](../../account/faq/#why-don-t-my-github-contributions-show-up).

## My name is displaying incorrectly. How do I fix it?

Open [**Profile & Account**](/profile), select **Edit profile**, update your **First Name** and **Last Name** fields, and select **Save Changes**.

This changes your name in LFX Self Serve only. It does not change the name on a training certificate that has already been issued. To correct a certificate, see [The name on my certificate is wrong](../../trainings/faq/#the-name-on-my-certificate-is-wrong-how-do-i-change-it).

## Where do I manage my email, password, affiliations, or CLAs?

Those live in the [Account](../../account/) section, not your personal profile. See the [Account FAQ](../../account/faq/) for email and password changes, affiliations, CLAs, transactions, and developer settings.

## Who do I contact if I cannot access my account?

Contact LFX support. If you cannot sign in at all, use the login page's help or forgotten password link.
