Public profile settings control whether your [public profile](../public-profile/) is visible and which sections it exposes. Your profile is private by default — nothing is shown publicly until you make it public.

## Open public profile settings

1. Sign in to [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev) and select [**Profile & Account**](/profile).
2. In the profile panel on the right, select **Public profile settings**. A drawer opens from the right side.

## Make your profile public or private

At the top of the drawer, **Profile visibility** has two options:

- **Private** (default) — your profile is hidden and no sections are exposed.
- **Public** — your profile is visible at `app.lfx.dev/u/<username>`.

When you switch to **Public**, a link to your public profile appears with buttons to **copy** it or **open** it in a new tab. Switching back to **Private** hides your profile and turns every section off.

## Choose which sections appear

When your profile is public, you control each section with a toggle. Turning a section off removes it from your public profile even if you have data for it. Settings are organized into two tabs.

### Sections tab

| Toggle                    | What it shows                                        |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **Project contributions** | Commits, pull requests, and issues across projects.  |
| **Committees & groups**   | Committee and group memberships and roles.           |
| **Event speaking**        | Talks and sessions you have presented at events.     |
| **Training enrollment**   | Training programs you have enrolled in or completed. |
| **Certifications**        | Certifications you have earned.                      |
| **Badges**                | Credentialing badges earned across projects.         |
| **Skills**                | Skills listed on your profile.                       |

### Personal Data tab

| Toggle                          | What it shows                                                      |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **General profile information** | Name, title, affiliation, and avatar shown on your public profile. |
| **About me**                    | Your long-form bio.                                                |
| **Personal information**        | Location and other personal details.                               |

**About me** and **Personal information** sit under **General profile information** — turning the general information off also turns those two off.

When you first make your profile public, **General profile information**, **About me**, and **Personal information** are turned on for you. You can adjust any of them afterward.

> **Note:** Some toggles are available in the drawer but do not yet add anything to your public profile page. Today the sections that actually appear are **General profile information**, **About me**, **Project contributions**, **Training enrollment**, **Certifications**, and **Badges**. **Committees & groups**, **Event speaking**, **Skills**, and **Personal information** can be enabled but are not shown on the public page yet.

## Saving changes

There is no Save button — your changes save automatically. A short status line shows **Saving changes…** and then **All changes saved**. Changes take effect the next time your public profile is regenerated, so they may not appear on your public page instantly.
