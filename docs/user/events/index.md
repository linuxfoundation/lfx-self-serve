The Events section shows Linux Foundation events relevant to you, your company, and your foundation. What you see depends on the lens you are in: your own registrations in the Me lens, your company's event footprint in the Organization lens, and a foundation's events in the Foundation lens.

## Your personal events

Select **Events** from the left navigation sidebar in the Me lens (`/events`). The page header reads _Events you're registered for, plus upcoming events from projects you're affiliated with._

### What you see

Three stat cards sit above the list:

- **Registered Events** — how many events you are registered for, with a **Next:** line naming your next one
- **Attended Events** — how many events you attended
- **Upcoming Events** — how many events are on the **Upcoming** tab

Below them, the **Upcoming**, **Past**, [**Visa Letters**](./visa-letters/), and [**Travel Funding**](./travel-funding/) tabs. The **Upcoming** tab includes events from your affiliated projects that you are not registered for yet, each with a **Register** button that opens the event's registration page. See [Browse Events](./browse-events/).

### Can't see an event you registered for?

Hover **Can't see your registered event?** in the page header: _Event registrations might take a while to show up. Just registered? It may take up to 1 hour to appear in your list._

### What you can do

- Browse your upcoming and past events, and register for upcoming events from your projects
- Search, sort, and filter by **Foundation**, **Role**, and **Status**
- Find more events with **Discover Events**, which opens [events.linuxfoundation.org](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/) in a new tab
- [Request a visa support letter](./visa-letters/) for an event you are registered for
- [Apply for travel funding](./travel-funding/) to attend an event
- [Download an attendance certificate](./attendance-certificates/) after you attend an event

### Who this applies to

Every signed-in user has a personal Events page.

## Your company's events

In the Organization lens, **Events** (`/org/events`) shows your company's event footprint across LF events — upcoming and past events, how many of your people are registered and speaking, and which events your company sponsors. See [Events and Event Attendees](../organization/org-events/) in the Organization docs.

## Your foundation's events

In the Foundation lens, **Events** (`/foundation/events`) lists the events hosted by the selected foundation and its ecosystem, with their registration status. See [Foundation Events](./foundation-events/).
