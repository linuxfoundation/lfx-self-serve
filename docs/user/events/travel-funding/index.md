The Travel Fund helps open-source developers and community members attend Linux Foundation events they would otherwise be unable to attend due to a lack of funding. Priority is given to applicants from underrepresented groups and those from lower-socioeconomic backgrounds. You apply for travel funding from the **Travel Funding** tab on the Events page.

> **Note:** If your employer is able to cover the cost of attending the event, please do not apply — the fund is intended for community members who have no other way to attend.

## Steps

1. Sign in to [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev).
2. Select **Events** from the left navigation sidebar.
3. Open the **Travel Funding** tab.
4. Select **New Funding Application**.
5. **Choose an Event** — select the event you want to attend, then select **Next Step**. The list shows upcoming events that offer travel funding and are still accepting applications. You do not need to be registered for the event.
6. **Terms and Conditions** — review them and select **I Agree**.
7. **About Me** — fill in your details (see [About Me fields](#about-me-fields)), then select **Next Step**.
8. **Expenses** — enter your estimated costs (see [Expenses fields](#expenses-fields)).
9. Select **Submit Application**. Use **Previous Step** to go back and change an earlier step.

### About Me fields

Fields marked `*` are required.

- **First Name** \* and **Last Name** \*
- **Email**
- **What country are you a citizen of?** \*
- **Link to your LinkedIn, personal website or GitHub page** \*
- **Company / Organization** \* — search for your organization
- **Can you receive funds from your organization?** \*
- **What country would you be travelling from?** \*
- **Briefly describe your involvement in open source communities and why you'd like to attend** \*
- **Do you belong to an underrepresented and/or marginalized group? Check all that apply:** — **LGBTQIA+**, **Woman**, **Person with Disability**, **Other**, or **Prefer not to answer** (optional)
- **Are you attending on behalf of a company?** \*
- **Are you willing to write a blog about the event?** \*
- **How many nights of accommodation do you need?** \* — a whole number, up to 4 nights

### Expenses fields

The **Estimated Travel Expenses** step asks for your best estimate in each category. All amounts are in USD, and each row has a cost and a **Notes** field:

- **Airfare (coach)** — for example, your departure city and airline
- **Hotel accommodation** — note the number of nights (max 4)
- **Ground transportation** — airport transfers

The step adds up an **Estimated Total:**, which must be greater than $0. A reminder underneath reads: _Lower cost requests are more likely to be approved. Funds may not be used for food, visa costs, non-airport transportation, or baggage fees._

## What travel funding can cover

- Coach-class airfare
- Accommodation for the event dates, plus one night before the event, up to 4 nights
- Ground transportation to and from the airport

## What travel funding does not cover

- Meals and food
- Visa costs
- Baggage fees
- Ground transportation that is not to or from the airport

## Approval

Applications are reviewed by the Linux Foundation events team. A few things to keep in mind:

- Funding decisions are final.
- Applicants are generally not funded for multiple events in the same year, or for two years running.
- Lower-cost requests are more likely to be approved, so keep your estimated expenses as reasonable as possible.

## Application status

After you submit, your request appears on the **Travel Funding** tab with its current **Status**: **Submitted**, **Approved**, **Denied**, or **Expired**. Each entry also shows the event name, location, application date, and deadline. Use **Search travel funding...** or the **Status** filter to find a request. Until you submit one, the tab shows _No travel funding requests yet_.

## Start from a link

A link to `/events?tab=travel-funding&event=<event id>` opens the Travel Funding tab and starts an application for that event. If the event isn't accepting travel fund applications, the dialog says **Event not found** and asks you to choose an event from the list instead.

## Can't start an application?

If **New Funding Application** is disabled and a message says _Your account is missing the required Salesforce ID. Please contact support._, select **Contact Support** in the message. LFX support needs to fix your account before you can apply.

## Questions

For questions about travel funding, contact the events team at [travelfund@linuxfoundation.org](mailto:travelfund@linuxfoundation.org).
