Your mentee profile is what program mentors read when they review your application. Keep it current while you have applications open.

## Before you begin

Sign in at [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev) and go to **Mentorship → Mentee → Mentee Profile**.

## Steps

1. Open [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee/profile](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee/profile).
2. Edit your introduction.
3. Adjust **skills you have** and **skills you want** with the skills picker. Up to 100 skills, each up to 300 characters.
4. Save.

## After completing

The change applies to your open applications straight away. It does not change an application you have already been accepted or declined on.
