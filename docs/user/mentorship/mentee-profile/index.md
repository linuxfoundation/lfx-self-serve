Your mentee profile is what program mentors read when they review your application. Keep it current while you have applications open.

## Before you begin

Sign in at [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev) and go to **Mentorship → Mentee → Mentee Profile**.

## What the tab shows

- **From Your LFX Profile** — your name, email and other account details. See [Your LFX Profile Card](../lfx-profile-card/).
- **Mentee Profile** — your **About Me**, **Skills**, **Areas to Improve**, **Additional Notes** and **Country of residence**.
- **Application History** — every program you have applied to.

## Steps

1. Open [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee/profile](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee/profile).
2. Select **Edit Mentee Profile**. A panel opens on the right.
3. Edit **About Me**. It is required.
4. Under **Skills**, adjust **What skills are you currently proficient in?** and **What areas do you want to improve in?** from the skills list. Up to 100 skills, each up to 300 characters.
5. Edit **Anything else you want mentors to know?** if you wish. It shows on your profile as **Additional Notes**, up to 1,000 characters.
6. Check your **Country of residence**. It is required.
7. Select **Save Changes**.

You can also open **Edit Mentee Profile** from the apply page, so you can fix your profile before you submit.

Your demographics answers are not edited here. Change them with **Edit Demographics** on the apply page — see [Apply to a Program](../apply-to-a-program/).

## After completing

The change applies to your open applications straight away. It does not change an application you have already been accepted or declined on.

## Application History

**Application History** lists each application with its program, term, submitted date and status:

| Status           | What it means                  |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **In Review**    | Submitted, awaiting a decision |
| **Accepted**     | You have a place on the term   |
| **Not Selected** | Not taken on for this term     |
| **Withdrawn**    | You pulled the application     |
| **Graduated**    | You finished the term          |
| **On Hold**      | Parked by the program          |

Each row has up to two actions:

- **View program** (eye icon) — opens the program's public page in a new tab.
- **Withdraw application** (bin icon) — shown only on an application that is **In Review**. See [Withdraw an Application](../withdraw-an-application/).
