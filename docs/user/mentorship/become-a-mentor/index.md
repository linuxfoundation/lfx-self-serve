Registering creates your mentor profile. Program Admins and applicants both read it, and it has to exist before you can join a program.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to LFX Self Serve at [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev) with your Linux Foundation account.
- Have your introduction and your skills ready, and know which programs you want to mentor.

## Steps

1. Go to [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor). The **Become a Mentor** form opens. Fields marked **\*** are required.
2. Check the **From Your LFX Profile** card at the top. It shows the details your mentor profile takes from your LFX account. Use **Edit LFX Profile** to change them, or **Connect** to link your GitHub or LinkedIn account. See [Your LFX Profile Card](../lfx-profile-card/).
3. Under **Mentorship Program Details**, open **Select LFX Mentorship** and pick a program. You can type to search, and pick more than one. Each pick is listed in the table below with the status **Pending**. To drop a pick, select **Withdraw** on its row and confirm.
4. Under **Introduction**, fill in **Tell us a little bit about yourself.** (required). The placeholder suggests what to cover: your contributor experience, why you want to mentor, and what makes you unique. Up to 3,000 characters.
5. Under **Skills**, answer **Which of your skills should we feature?** (required). Type or choose a skill and select **Add Skill**. Add at least one skill, and up to 100 skills of up to 300 characters each. Select the **×** on a skill to remove it.
6. Under **Compliance Confirmation**, tick the box (required). It certifies that you, and any organization you represent, are not located in one of the listed countries or regions, not acting for one of the listed governments, and not listed as a blocked person by the U.S. Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC). The form names the countries, regions and governments.
7. Under **Terms and Conditions**, tick the box to agree to the LFX Platform Use Agreement, the Service-Specific Use Terms, the Acceptable Use Policy and the Privacy Policy (required).
8. Select **Submit**.

The program list is optional. Your picks are only sent once your profile is saved; each one then goes to that program's administrator as a request.

## After completing

You see **Profile created** and land on **My Programs**. The Mentor area has two tabs: **My Programs** and **Mentor Profile**.

A program you requested does not appear on **My Programs** until a Program Admin approves the request. If a request cannot be sent, you see **Could not send the request** for that program.

If you already have a mentor profile, returning to [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor) takes you straight to **My Programs** rather than showing the form again.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see                                                     | What it means                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Check your registration"                                        | A required field is empty, the introduction is too long, or a skill exceeds the limits. |
| "You already have a mentor profile, so we did not overwrite it." | Your profile already exists. Select **Go to My Programs**.                              |
| "Your profile is in conflict with an existing record."           | Refresh the page and try again.                                                         |
| "You are viewing as another user, so registration is read-only." | You are in a view-as session; leave it and register as yourself.                        |
