Registering creates your mentor profile. Program Admins and applicants both read it, and it has to exist before you can join a program.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to LFX Self Serve at [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev) with your Linux Foundation account.
- Have your introduction, your skills and your project links ready.

## Steps

1. Go to [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor).
2. Fill in the registration form, including your introduction and your skills. Up to 100 skills, each up to 300 characters.
3. Acknowledge the platform terms.
4. Submit the form.

## After completing

You land on the Mentor area, which has two tabs: **My Programs** and **Mentor Profile**.

If you already have a mentor profile, returning to the Mentor area takes you straight to **My Programs** rather than showing the form again.
