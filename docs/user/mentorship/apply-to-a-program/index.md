The apply page confirms your profile and your eligibility, then submits your application for the term. Anything the program wants you to produce comes afterwards, as prerequisite tasks.

![The apply page, showing the LFX profile and mentee profile used for the application](/images/docs/mentorship/apply-to-a-program.png)

## Before you begin

- You need a mentee profile. See [Become a Mentee](../become-a-mentee/).
- You may hold at most **three** open applications at a time. Withdrawing one frees a slot.
- Find the program first at [mentorship.linuxfoundation.org](https://mentorship.linuxfoundation.org) and select **Apply to This Program**; the link carries the program and term you are applying to.
- The term must still be accepting applications.

## Steps

1. Open the apply page from the program's **Apply to This Program** button. The page is headed **Apply to** and the program's name.
2. Check the **From Your LFX Profile** card. Your name, email and picture are used from your LFX account. Select **Edit LFX Profile** to change them. See [Your LFX Profile Card](../lfx-profile-card/).
3. Check **Your Mentee Profile**. This is what the mentors reviewing your application read. Select **Edit Mentee Profile** to change it without leaving the page. See [Edit Your Mentee Profile](../mentee-profile/).
4. Check **Demographics**. It is optional, and never shared with mentors. Select **Edit Demographics** to change your answers in a side panel, then select **Save Changes**.
5. Under **Before You Apply**, tick each statement:
   - You are old enough to take part.
   - You are authorized to work.
   - You do not already hold a duplicate mentee profile.
   - The compliance statement.
   - The **Terms and Conditions**. If you are accepted, the program may share parts of your profile to publicize your participation.
6. Select **Submit Application**.

The apply page collects no cover letter, resume or file. It confirms your profile and the five statements, and submits the program and term. Anything the program asks you to produce is assigned as a prerequisite task **after** the application exists.

**Submit Application** stays disabled until all five statements are ticked. A counter beside it shows how many remain.

If you have just registered, **Before You Apply** is not shown. You confirmed the same statements when you registered, so you can submit straight away.

## After completing

You land on your **Overview** tab and see "Application submitted". The application appears there as a card. Its badge is **In Progress** while prerequisite tasks remain, and **Awaiting Review** once you have submitted them all — or straight away if the program assigned none.

Any prerequisite tasks appear under **My Tasks**, grouped as **Prerequisite Tasks**. Work through those next: the program reviews your application once they are in.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see                                         | What it means                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The page says the term is not accepting applications | The term closed between opening the program page and applying. Pick another program or wait for the next term. |
| You cannot reach the apply page                      | You have no mentee profile yet, or the link is missing the program or term. Start again from the program page. |
| **Submit Application** stays disabled                | One of the five confirmations is unticked.                                                                     |
