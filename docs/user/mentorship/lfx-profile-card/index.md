The **From Your LFX Profile** card shows the account details your mentorship profiles use. You do not type them into the mentorship forms. They come from your LFX account, and you fix them from the card.

## Where you see it

The card sits at the top of these pages:

| Page                   | Where                                                                                  |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Become a Mentor**    | [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor)                 |
| **Mentor Profile** tab | [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor/profile](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentor/profile) |
| **Become a Mentee**    | [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee)                 |
| The apply page         | Opened from a program's **Apply to This Program** button                               |
| **Mentee Profile** tab | [app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee/profile](https://app.lfx.dev/mentorship/mentee/profile) |

## What it shows

- Your picture and name.
- Your email addresses. Your primary address is marked **Primary**.
- **Mailing Address** and **Phone**.
- **GitHub** and **LinkedIn** — the account you have connected, or a **Connect** button.

A field your LFX profile leaves empty shows "Not provided".

## Edit your LFX profile

1. On the card, select **Edit LFX Profile**. The **Edit Profile** panel opens on the right. You stay on the mentorship page.
2. Change the details you want.
3. Select **Save Changes**.

The card updates straight away. Your new name, email and picture are also copied onto your mentor and mentee profiles, if you have them. On a register page, the details on the card when you select **Submit** are the ones your new profile uses.

The panel is the same one used under **Profile & Account**. See [Edit Your Profile](../../profile/edit-profile/) for its fields and limits.

## Connect GitHub or LinkedIn

**Connect** appears on the **GitHub** or **LinkedIn** row when you have not connected that account yet.

1. Select **Connect**. The **Add identity** dialog opens.
2. Select **Continue with GitHub** (or **Continue with LinkedIn**).
3. Sign in to that account and approve the link.

You leave the page to sign in, then come back to the same page with the account on the card and the message "Identity linked successfully." A connected GitHub account is also added to your mentorship profiles as a link.

Anything you have typed into a form on the page and not yet submitted is lost when you leave to sign in. Connect your accounts before you fill in a register form.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see                                              | What it means                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Edit LFX Profile** is disabled                          | Your profile could not be loaded. Reload the page.                                                                                |
| **Connect** is disabled                                   | You are viewing as another user. Connecting would link the account to you, not to them.                                           |
| No **Connect** button, and the row says "Not provided"    | Your connected accounts could not be loaded. Reload the page.                                                                     |
| "That account is already linked to another LFX profile."  | Open **Profile & Account → Identities** to sort it out. See [Identities](../../account/identities/).                              |
| "Authorization finished, but the account was not linked." | Select **Connect** to try again.                                                                                                  |
| "Mentorship profile not updated"                          | Your LFX profile or account saved, but your mentorship profile still shows the old details. Save your LFX profile again to retry. |
