A Program Admin can invite you to mentor their program. The invitation arrives by email and is answered on its own page in LFX Self Serve.

## Before you begin

- Use the link in the invitation email. The link identifies your invitation.
- You need a mentor profile to answer. If you don't have one yet, create it first.
- If the email was sent to you because you don't have an LF account yet, follow its three steps in order: create an LF account with the email address the invitation was sent to, create your mentor profile, then answer the invitation. The invitation only works for an LF account that uses that address.

## Steps

1. Open the invitation email and select its link.
2. Sign in at [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev) if you are not already signed in. The page reads **You've been invited to be a mentor** and names the program in bold. Select **View program** to open the program's public page in a new tab.
3. Select **Accept** or **Decline**.

The page only records your answer when you select one of those buttons, so a mail scanner that follows the link cannot answer on your behalf.

If you don't have a mentor profile yet, **Accept** and **Decline** are disabled, and the page links to **Create your mentor profile**. Create your profile, then open the invitation link again.

You can also answer from **Mentor Profile → Edit Mentor Profile**, where the invitation is listed with the status **Invited**. See [Your Mentor Programs](../mentor-programs/). An invitation sent to an email address before you had an LF account is not listed there; answer it from the email link.

## After completing

- **Accept** shows **Invitation accepted** and names the program you now mentor. Select **Go to My Programs** to see the program on your **My Programs** tab.
- **Decline** shows **Invitation declined** and lets the program admins know.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see                                 | What it means                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "This invitation is for a different account" | You are signed in with a different LF account from the one the email was sent to, or, for an invitation sent to an email address, with an LF account that doesn't use that address. Sign in with the right account and open the link again. |
| "This invitation link can't be used"         | The invitation was already answered or revoked. The page can show this as soon as it opens. Ask the Program Admin to invite you again.                                                                                                      |
| "You're viewing as another user"             | You are in a view-as session. Leave it, then open the link again.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| "Something went wrong"                       | Your answer was not recorded. Select **Try again**.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| "We couldn't check your mentor profile"      | The page could not confirm your mentor profile, so **Accept** and **Decline** stay disabled. Select **Try again**.                                                                                                                          |
