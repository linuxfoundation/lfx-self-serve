The Crowdfunding section lets you manage the fundraising initiatives you own and track the donations you have made to Linux Foundation projects. It is organized into two areas: **Initiatives** for initiative owners and **Donations** for contributors.

## What you can do

- View and manage crowdfunding initiatives you have created
- Monitor funding stats including total raised and sponsor count
- Track your donation history across one-time and recurring contributions
- Manage saved payment methods
- Cancel or review recurring donation subscriptions
- View the initiatives run on behalf of a project, foundation, or organization you write to

## Who this applies to

All authenticated users can access the Crowdfunding section. **Initiatives** is relevant to users who have created or submitted crowdfunding initiatives. **Donations** is relevant to users who have contributed to any initiative.

Users with write access to a project, foundation, or organization also see that entity's initiatives under its lens — see [Lens-scoped Initiatives](./lens-initiatives/).

## Navigation

Go to **app.lfx.dev** and select **Crowdfunding** from the left navigation sidebar under the **Me** lens. The section expands to show **Initiatives** ([app.lfx.dev/crowdfunding/initiatives](https://app.lfx.dev/crowdfunding/initiatives)) and **Donations** ([app.lfx.dev/crowdfunding/donations](https://app.lfx.dev/crowdfunding/donations)).

Under the **Project**, **Foundation**, and **Org** lenses, select **Crowdfunding Initiatives** from the left navigation sidebar to see the initiatives run on behalf of the selected project, foundation, or organization. This item appears only if you can write to that project or foundation, or have a writer role granted directly on that organization.

## Key concepts

- **Initiative**: A fundraising campaign for an open-source project. Initiatives have a type (General Fund, Security Audit, Mentorship, or Event) and a status that determines their visibility and whether they accept donations.
- **Initiative status**: Initiatives move through the following statuses:
  - **Published** (shown under the Active filter) — the initiative is live and accepting donations
  - **Pending** — the initiative has been created and is awaiting review before going live
  - **Submitted** — the initiative has been submitted for final approval; still under review
  - **Hidden** (shown under the Archived filter) — the initiative has been archived and no longer accepts donations
  - **Declined** (shown under the Archived filter) — the initiative was not approved during review
- **Recurring donation**: A monthly subscription that automatically charges a saved payment method each billing cycle. A recurring donation subscription can be Active, Paused, or Canceled. Canceled subscriptions stay in your list with a **Canceled** status.
- **Paused recurring donation**: A recurring donation subscription that is temporarily on hold. No charges are processed while a subscription is paused.
- **One-time donation**: A single charge made to an initiative without a recurring subscription.
- **Payment method**: A saved credit or debit card used to process donations.
