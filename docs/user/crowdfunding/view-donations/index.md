The View Donations page gives you a complete picture of your crowdfunding contributions. It shows a stats bar, your recurring donations, a paginated table of all your donation history, and your saved payment methods.

## Stats bar

At the top of the page, a stats bar summarizes your contribution activity:

- **Total Donated** — the cumulative amount you have contributed across all initiatives
- **Initiatives Supported** — the number of distinct initiatives you have donated to
- **Active Recurring** — your total monthly recurring commitment and the count of active subscriptions

## Recurring donations

Below the stats bar, the **Recurring Donations** section lists all your recurring donation subscriptions, including canceled ones. It appears only when you have at least one recurring donation. Each entry shows:

- Initiative name and logo
- Status: **Active** (green), **Paused** (gray), or **Canceled** (red)
- Start date and billing description
- Monthly amount
- **Next charge** date for an active subscription, or **Paused since** for a paused one
- An **Anonymous** tag when you have hidden your name on the subscription

Select a recurring donation to open its detail page, where you can review the full charge history. The **More options** menu (⋯) on each entry offers **Hide my name** or **Show my name** (see [Hide or show your name](#hide-or-show-your-name)) and, unless the subscription is already canceled, **Cancel**. See [Manage Recurring Donations](../manage-recurring-donations/).

## Donation history

The **Donation History** table lists all your donations in chronological order, including both recurring and one-time contributions. Each row shows:

- Initiative name, with a **Recurring** tag for recurring contributions and an **Anonymous** tag when your name is hidden on the donation
- Donation date
- Amount
- A **More options** menu (⋯) with **Hide my name** or **Show my name**, on donations whose name visibility can be changed

Select **Load more** to see older donations.

## Hide or show your name

When you donate on LFX Crowdfunding you can choose to donate anonymously, and you can change that choice later from this page. An anonymous donation still appears, with its amount, everywhere donations are listed (the initiative's public page, the initiative team's financials, and an organization's donations list), but your name and email are replaced by **Anonymous**. If you donated on behalf of an organization, the organization's name and logo still show; only your name is hidden. Nobody but you can see that the donation is yours: here, in your own donation history, you always see it with your name and the **Anonymous** tag.

To hide or show your name on a donation:

1. Go to **Crowdfunding → Donations**.
2. Find the donation in **Donation History**, or the subscription in **Recurring Donations**.
3. Select the **More options** menu (⋯) and choose **Hide my name** or **Show my name**.

A confirmation message appears and the **Anonymous** tag is added or removed. For a recurring donation the setting covers the whole subscription: changing it on the subscription, or on any one of its charges, applies to every charge, past and future. You can also change it from the recurring donation's detail page (see [Manage Recurring Donations](../manage-recurring-donations/#hide-or-show-your-name-on-a-recurring-donation)).

Some older donations have no **More options** menu. Their name visibility can't be changed from LFX Self Serve; contact LFX support if you need it changed.

## Payment methods

The **Payment Methods** section at the bottom of the page shows your saved card. See [Manage Payment Methods](../manage-payment-methods/).

## Find an initiative to support

Select **Explore Initiatives** in the page header to open the initiative catalog in the separate [LFX Crowdfunding product](https://crowdfunding.linuxfoundation.org) in a new tab. When your **Donation History** is empty, it also offers **Explore Initiatives**.

You can browse the catalog without having made a previous donation. Choose an initiative there to learn more and donate; donations happen in LFX Crowdfunding, not in LFX Self Serve. Return to **Donations** to review your giving history and manage recurring contributions.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to LFX Self Serve at [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev) with your Linux Foundation account.
- No previous donation is required to open this page or use **Explore Initiatives**. Your stats and history reflect your contributions once you have donated.

## Steps

1. Sign in to [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev).
2. Select **Crowdfunding → Donations** from the left navigation sidebar.
3. Review the stats bar for an overview of your contribution activity.
4. Scroll down to the **Recurring Donations** list to see your subscriptions and their status.
5. Scroll further to the donation history table to see all past contributions.
6. Select a recurring donation to open its detail page.

## After completing

Your full donation history is visible in the table. Selecting a recurring donation opens its detail page where you can review charge history or cancel the subscription — see [Manage Recurring Donations](../manage-recurring-donations/).
