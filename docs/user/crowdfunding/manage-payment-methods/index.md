Your saved payment card is shown in the **Payment Methods** section at the bottom of the Donations page. It shows the card brand, the last four digits, and the expiry date. You can add a card or remove the saved one at any time.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to LFX Self Serve at [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev) with your Linux Foundation account.
- Before removing a card: check whether it is linked to any active recurring donations. Removing it will cause those donations to fail on the next billing cycle. To avoid missed charges, cancel the recurring donation first — see [Manage Recurring Donations](../manage-recurring-donations/).

## Add a payment card

1. Sign in to [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev).
2. Select **Crowdfunding → Donations** from the left navigation sidebar.
3. Scroll to the **Payment Methods** section at the bottom of the page.
4. Select **Add payment card**.
5. In the **Add Payment Card** dialog, enter your card number, expiry date, and CVC (the 3-digit security code on the back of the card).
6. Select **Add card**.

## After completing (add)

The dialog closes and the new card appears in the **Payment Methods** section. It can be used for future donations.

## Remove a payment method

> **Before removing:** If you remove a card that is linked to an active recurring donation, that donation will fail on the next billing cycle because there is no longer a valid payment method to charge. To avoid a missed charge, cancel the recurring donation before removing the card — see [Manage Recurring Donations](../manage-recurring-donations/).

1. Sign in to [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev).
2. Select **Crowdfunding → Donations** from the left navigation sidebar.
3. Scroll to the **Payment Methods** section at the bottom of the page.
4. Select the trash icon (**Remove payment method**) on the card.
5. In the **Remove Payment Method** dialog, select **Remove**. This cannot be undone.

## After completing (remove)

The card is removed from your account immediately. Any active recurring donations linked to that card will fail on their next billing cycle — cancel them before removing the card if you want to stop future charges cleanly.

## Frequently asked

**Will removing a payment method cancel my recurring donations?**
No. Removing a card does not automatically cancel any recurring donations linked to it. Those donations will fail on the next billing cycle because there is no valid payment method to charge. To stop future charges, cancel the recurring donation before removing the card — see [Manage Recurring Donations](../manage-recurring-donations/).

**Can I add the same card again after removing it?**
Yes. You can re-add any card at any time by selecting **Add payment card** and entering the card details again.
