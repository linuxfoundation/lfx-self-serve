## Who can create a group?

You need write access to the selected foundation or project (executive directors always have it), and you create groups from the **Foundation** or **Project** lens. **Create Group** isn't shown in the **Me** lens. Contributors can view groups and join or leave them where the join mode allows. See [Create a Group](../create-committee/).

## How do I join a group?

It depends on the group's join mode. In an **Open** group, select **Join Group**. In an **Apply & Review** group, select **Apply to Join** and optionally add a message. In an **Invite Only** group you need an invitation, and in a **Closed** group only admins add members. See [Join, Apply to, and Leave a Group](../join-and-leave-a-group/).

## I applied to join. What happens next?

Your badge shows **Application Pending** and the group's managers see your request in **Pending Applications** on the Members tab. They approve or reject it, and you're notified. You can't apply again while one is pending.

## I was invited to a group. How do I accept?

Open the group. A banner at the top has **Accept** and **Decline**. You can also respond from your pending actions on the dashboard. After you select **Decline**, you have about five seconds to select **Undo**.

## How do I leave a group?

Open the group and select **Leave Group** in the header. It takes effect immediately and doesn't ask you to confirm.

## Why can't I see a group I should be in?

Contact LFX support and tell us which group you were expecting to see. If you're signed in, we can already see your account, so that's all we need from you.

Two things cause this: either you're not currently on that group's list, or you are and our records haven't linked you to your login yet. Support will check which and follow up. Your group's chair can also confirm your membership and send you meeting details directly in the meantime.

## Why can't I see the member list or some tabs?

What you see depends on your access and the group's settings. Managers and auditors always see the member list. Everyone else sees it only when the group's **Member Visibility** is **Basic Profile**. **Votes** appears only when voting is enabled, **Meetings** is shown to visitors only when the group's calendar is public, and **Settings** is for managers and auditors. See [Manage Groups](../manage-committees/#the-group-page).

## How do I add members to a group?

Open the group, go to the **Members** tab, and select **Add Member** to search and add by name or email. In an Invite Only group, members who aren't managers see **Invite Someone** instead. See [Manage Groups](../manage-committees/#add-or-remove-members).

## Can I add many members at once?

Yes. If you can manage the group's members, use **Import Members** on the group's **Members** tab to add up to 500 people from a CSV file, including each person's name, organization, role, and voting details. The import adds people directly as members after you review a preview — it does not send invitations, change anyone's permissions, or create groups. You can also select **Import Members** on the **Add Members** step when creating or editing a group; there, members stay pending in the editable list until you select **Done** or **Update Group**. See [Import members from a CSV](../manage-committees/#import-members-from-a-csv).

**Add Member** is quicker for a few people: you can paste email addresses or import a meeting's registrants, but groups that require an organization need one person at a time there. Members who can only send invitations see **Invite Someone** instead and cannot import a CSV.

## Can I have the same person in multiple groups?

Yes, a single user can be a member of multiple groups within the same project or across different projects.

## How do I change a group's name, type, or join mode?

Open the group and use **Edit Group**, or open its **Settings** tab and select **Save Changes**. Changing the group type asks you to confirm first. See [Group Settings](../group-settings/).

## How do I delete a group?

Managers can delete a group themselves. Open the group's **Settings** tab, select **Delete Group** in the **Danger Zone**, and confirm. Deleting permanently removes the group and its meetings, votes, surveys, and member records, and can't be undone. If you can't see **Delete Group**, ask a manager of the group or contact LFX support.

## How do I link a mailing list to a group?

On the group's **Settings** tab, select **+ Add List** under **Associated Mailing Lists**. Members are subscribed automatically when they join. See [Group Settings](../group-settings/#associated-mailing-lists).

## Who do I contact if I need help with groups?

Contact LFX support. Include your project name and the group name in your request.
