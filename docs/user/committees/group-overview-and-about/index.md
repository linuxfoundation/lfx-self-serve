Every group's page opens on the **Overview** tab, next to the **About** tab. Both are visible to everyone who can open the group. What each person sees depends on their access to the group.

## Overview

### Counts

The Overview lists quick counts: **Members**, **Meetings**, **Active Votes**, and **Open Surveys**. People who can see the member list also see **Organizations** and **Voting Reps**.

### Chairs

People who can see the member list see the group's **Chairs** with their roles, or "No chairs assigned". Managers can select the pencil icon (**Edit chairs**) to change who the chairs are.

### Engagement summary

When engagement metrics are turned on for your account and you have access to them, an engagement summary appears below the counts. It isn't available to everyone yet, so you may not see it. When it's on, it also adds **Meetings** and **Engagement** columns to the Members tab.

### Next Meeting and Past Meeting

Two cards show the group's next upcoming meeting and its most recent one, each with **View All** to open the Meetings tab. Visitors see the cards only when the group's calendar is public, and without **View All**.

### My Pending Actions

Members see open votes and surveys they still need to respond to, with **View All** to see more.

### Weekly brief

Members with access to engagement data may also see an AI-written weekly brief card. It isn't available to everyone yet.

Managers can select **Email Group Members** to email the current brief to all members of the group. The email goes to the group's members, not the mailing list's subscribers, and it doesn't appear in the mailing list's history. The button is available only when the group has a mailing list configured, and only project writers can use it. Managers who aren't project writers see it disabled with a note to ask a project administrator. If sharing access can't be verified, the button stays disabled with an error message and a **Retry** action.

### Recent Activity

A feed of recent events in the group. Visitors see document events, plus meeting events when the group's calendar is public. Visitors who aren't members also see a prompt to join.

## About

### About and channels

The group's description, which managers can edit with the pencil icon (**Add description** when there isn't one yet). Below it, **Channels** lists the group's chat channel, website, and mailing lists. Visitors see only public mailing lists.

### Meeting Cadence

A short summary of how often the group meets, worked out from its upcoming meetings.

Select **Subscribe to Calendar** to add the group's meetings to your own calendar. The dialog gives you a calendar link with a **Copy** button, plus ready-made links for **Google Calendar**, **Outlook.com**, **Outlook 365 / Office**, and **Apple Calendar**.

### Part of

The project or foundation the group belongs to, its **parent group** if it has one, and any **sub-groups**. Select a name to open it.

### Key Information

**Category**, **Join Mode**, **Total Members**, **Voting Enabled**, and when the group was **Created** and last **Updated**.

### Charter

A link to the group's charter, or "No charter yet". Managers can select the pencil icon to **Add charter** or **Edit charter**.
