The Badges page shows the digital credentials you have earned through your participation in Linux Foundation projects and programs. Badges are issued and hosted by Credly, and LFX One displays the badges linked to your account.

## What you can do

- View all badges earned across your projects
- See who issued each badge, when it was issued, when it expires, and its credential ID
- Spot badges that are Pending, Expired, Public, or Private at a glance
- Filter by category, visibility, and status
- Accept a pending badge or view a public badge in Credly, and manage private-badge visibility through your Credly profile editor
- Share your public badges to LinkedIn or by link

## Who this applies to

All authenticated users can view their own badges. The badges displayed reflect the credentials Credly holds for the email addresses on your LFX account.

## Getting started

Go to **app.lfx.dev**, open the **Me** lens, and select **Badges** under **Education** in the left sidebar. Your earned badges appear in the main content area. Filter by category using the tabs: **All**, **Certifications**, **Learning**, **Memberships**, **Speaking**, **Event Participation**, **Contributors**, **Program Committee**.
