Your LFX badges are digital credentials that recognize your contributions and achievements across Linux Foundation projects and programs. Badges come from Credly, and you can view all of them in one place.

## Steps

1. Sign in to [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev).
2. In the **Me** lens, select **Badges** under **Education** in the left sidebar.
3. Your earned badges appear as cards. Use the tabs to filter by category: **All**, **Certifications**, **Learning**, **Memberships**, **Speaking**, **Event Participation**, **Contributors**, **Program Committee**.
4. Select **Filters** to narrow the list by visibility or status (see below).
5. Use the buttons on a card to open the badge in Credly, accept it, manage its privacy, or share it.

## What a badge card shows

- The badge image, title, and description
- The badge category
- **Issued by** — the issuer of the badge
- **Issued on** — the date the badge was issued
- **Expires on** (or **Expired on** once it has passed) — shown only for badges that have an expiry date
- **Credential ID**

Selecting the card itself follows the same Credly link as its main button. For an accepted Private badge, this opens your Credly profile editor, not the badge page.

## Status chips

Each card can show these chips:

- **Pending** — you have not yet accepted the badge in Credly
- **Expired** — the badge's expiry date has passed
- **Public** — the badge is visible to others through Credly
- **Private** — the badge is visible only to you

## Filters

Select **Filters** next to the category tabs to open the filter popover. A dot on the button means a filter is active.

- **Visibility** — **All**, **Public**, or **Private**
- **Status** — **All**, **Pending**, **Active**, or **Expired**

Filters combine with the category tabs.

## Card buttons

The main button on a card depends on the badge:

- **Accept Badge** — shown on a Pending badge. It opens the badge in Credly, where you accept it.
- **View in Credly** — shown on an accepted Public badge. It opens the badge in Credly.
- **Manage Privacy** — shown on an accepted Private badge. It opens your Credly profile editor, where you manage badge privacy.

## Share a badge

A **Share** button appears next to **View in Credly** only for badges that are Public and not Pending. Private and Pending badges cannot be shared from LFX One. The Share menu offers:

- **Add to LinkedIn** — opens LinkedIn's add-to-profile page for the badge
- **Share…** — opens your device's share sheet, when your browser supports it
- **Copy link** — copies the badge's public URL to your clipboard

## Pagination

Badges are shown 12 per page. A line above the cards reads "Showing X to Y of Z Badges", and page controls appear below the cards when you have more than 12 badges.

## Loading, empty, and error states

- While badges load, the page shows "Loading badges…" with placeholder cards.
- If no badges match your category, visibility, or status selection (or you have no badges), the page shows **No badges found** with the message "No badges match the selected filter. Try selecting a different category."
- If LFX One cannot load the Badges page data, the page shows **Unable to load badges** with the message "Something went wrong while fetching your badges. Please try again later." A temporary Credly problem can instead show **No badges found** or an incomplete list. Wait and reload later (see the [Badges FAQ](../faq/#why-don-t-i-see-a-badge-i-expected-to-receive) for cache timing).
