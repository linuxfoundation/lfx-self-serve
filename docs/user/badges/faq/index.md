## How do I earn a badge?

Badges are awarded when you complete the requirements for a Linux Foundation program, certification, or contribution milestone. You do not need to apply manually. Badges are issued through Credly, and you may need to accept a new badge there before it is fully yours.

## Where can I see all my badges?

In the **Me** lens of [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev), select **Badges** under **Education** in the left sidebar. All badges earned across your projects appear as cards.

## Why don't I see a badge I expected to receive?

First check whether the badge is waiting for you to accept it in Credly. Until you accept it, the badge shows a **Pending** chip, and you can use the filter's **Status** option to show only **Pending** badges. If you have not accepted it, select **Accept Badge** on the card to open it in Credly.

If the badge is not listed at all, check Credly first. LFX One caches badge results for 30 minutes by default, or five minutes when the cached results include Pending badges. After waiting, reload the Badges page to fetch the latest results. These cache durations do not guarantee when Credly will issue a badge. If it is still missing, contact LFX support with the badge name.

## Are my badges visible to others?

Each card shows a **Public** or **Private** chip. Visibility is managed in Credly. On an accepted Private badge, select **Manage Privacy** to open your Credly profile editor and manage badge privacy. If the badge is Pending, select **Accept Badge** and accept it first.

## Can I share my badges externally?

Only Public badges that are not Pending can be shared. These cards have a **Share** button with **Add to LinkedIn**, **Share…** (when your browser supports it), and **Copy link**. To share a Private badge, first make it public using **Manage Privacy**. If the badge is Pending, accept it first.

## Do badges expire?

Some badges have an expiry date. Cards for these badges show **Expires on** with the date, or **Expired on** and an **Expired** chip once the date has passed. Use the **Status** filter to show only **Expired** badges.

## Who do I contact if a badge is incorrect?

Contact LFX support. Include the badge name and the project it relates to so the support team can investigate.
