What happens next

  1. 1. The formation team reviews your proposal in the Formations queue.
  2. 2. Once accepted, the formation team sets up the project record and works through the formation checklist with you.
  3. 3. You can track progress from the project page as each checklist item is completed.

Fields like formation lead, coordinator, product contact, and announcement date aren't collected here — the formation team sets those in the admin tool, and they sync to this project's page automatically.
