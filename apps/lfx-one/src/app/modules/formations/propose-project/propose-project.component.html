Propose a project

Tell us about the project you want to bring to the Linux Foundation. This sends your proposal to the LF formation team for review. It does not commit you to anything yet.

What happens next

  1. 1
    The formation team gets your proposal Only you and the formation team can see it. You can revise or withdraw it while it's under review.
  2. 2
    Formation team accepts or denies Accepting creates the project in Formation and starts the checklist; contributor agreement and IP assignment are drafted from what you entered.
  3. 3
    You work the checklist together Slack, mailing list, and GitHub can be set up while legal review is in progress.
  4. 4
    Project becomes Active Once agreements are signed.

Questions?
